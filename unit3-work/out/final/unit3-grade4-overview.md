# Unit 3 · Grade 4 · Global Readings 3: "It Keeps Changing" (pp. 40-55)

**Big question (book p.55):** How can we make good changes in our world?

**Topic:** Climate change: changes in the Arctic (a poem about a man and a polar bear) and clean cars.

**Reading skill:** Compare and contrast (p.47) · **Values:** Social awareness · **Sound:** final -ng

## Readings
- **The Bear and I** (Fiction - a poem (8 stanzas of 4 lines, 2 stanzas per page), pp. 42-45)
- **Clean Cars** (Nonfiction - informational text, pp. 48-53)

## Objectives (planning, not printed in the book)
- Pupils will be able to retell the poem The Bear and I in 5 simple sentences (young man, bear takes fish, thin ice, fall in, ice is melting).
- Pupils will be able to compare and contrast two things (the man and the bear; two cars) using 'Both ...' and 'The bear ..., but the man ...'.
- Pupils will be able to describe 5 kinds of cars (gasoline, biofuel, electric, air, solar) with fast/slow, noisy/quiet, clean/dirty, expensive.
- Pupils will be able to give a reason with 'because' (I like the solar car because it's quiet.).
- Pupils will be able to say one change they can make to keep the world clean (I can ... because ...).
- Pupils will be able to read and say words ending in -ng (sing, long, spring, melting, fishing).

## Grammar in the printed text
- **Past simple: was / were and irregular verbs**: My fishing hole was in the ice, (p.42) · Each morning, fish were there. (p.42)
- **Present continuous for change now**: Today the ice is melting, (p.45) · And I am getting old, (p.45)
- **Present simple for facts**: Gasoline comes from oil in the ground. (p.49) · Solar cars use energy from the sun. (p.53)
- **Adjectives after be (They are ...), with and / but**: They are fast and quiet. (p.51) · They are clean, but they are expensive. (p.51)
- **not very / very + adjective**: They are not very expensive. (p.52) · They are very noisy. (p.50)
- **because (reason)**: I think they're not friends because the bear takes his fish. (p.46) · I can clean up trash because trash is a danger for animals. (p.55)
- **can / can be**: they can be expensive (p.53) · I can ride a bike to school (p.55)
- **There is / There are**: There are so many rocks! (p.41) · There is danger in this changing world, (p.45)
- **Comparing with 'Both' and 'but' (skill language, added)**: Not printed as sentences in the book. Suggested frames for p.47: 'Both the bear and the man eat fish.' 'The bear lives on the ice, but the man lives in a house.'

> The book does NOT use '-er than' comparatives or 'instead of' in this unit (earlier AI notes said it did; that was wrong).
