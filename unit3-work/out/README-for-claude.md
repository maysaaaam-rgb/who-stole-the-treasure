# Unit 3 Curriculum Handover & Architecture Notes for Claude

## 1. Five-Line Unit Summaries

### Grade 3: "How Much Is It?" (*Global Readings 2*, pp. 40–55)
1. Unit 3 explores the historical evolution of money from barter (6000 BCE) to cowrie shells, Lydia stamped coins, Chinese paper notes, and modern credit cards.
2. In Reading 2 ("The $100 Bill"), Paige struggles with peer pressure after seeing friends' smartphones, but discovers a wet $100 bill in the park leaves.
3. Paige overcomes the urge to buy a phone, recognizing her family's tight budget, and treats her exhausted dad to a warm pizza dinner.
4. The phonics focus teaches the spelling pattern **ar** (/ɑːr/ sound: *car, park, hard, farm, card, smart*).
5. Core language structures practice price questions (*"How much is it? / It is..."*), modals for ethical choices (*could* vs *should*), and the idiom *"money is tight"*.

### Grade 4: "It Keeps Changing" (*Global Readings 3*, pp. 40–55)
1. Unit 3 investigates environmental change, beginning with Arctic climate change and moving to clean green vehicle technologies.
2. Reading 1 ("The Bear and I") is an evocative narrative poem where an Arctic fisherman and a hungry polar bear find themselves trapped together on thin, cracking sea ice.
3. Reading 2 ("Clean Cars") contrasts noisy, polluting gasoline cars with eco-friendly alternatives: plant-based biofuel, battery-powered electric cars, and solar vehicles.
4. The phonics focus develops mastery over the final consonant blend **-ng** (/ŋ/ sound: *sing, long, reading, bang, spring*).
5. Core language structures target comparative adjectives (*cleaner, quieter, more expensive*), cause-and-effect with *because* and *so*, and contrast using *instead of*.

## 4. Smartboard Classroom Activity Recommendations (Single Projector / Smartboard)

### 1. Interactive Arcade Mini-Games
- **Grade 3 Barter Market Match**: Two students tap and swap goods (grain, shells, coins) to purchase supplies before the timer expires.
- **Grade 4 Clean Energy Drive**: Students tap to catch sun rays and battery bolts while avoiding gasoline exhaust clouds.

### 2. Live Karaoke Teleprompter & Reader's Theatre
- **Grade 3 Paige's Big Choice**: Two students read roles (Paige vs Dad) with highlighted teleprompter pacing and instant pronunciation feedback.
- **Grade 4 The Bear and I (Poetry Performance)**: Call-and-response choral reading of the poem with dynamic text size pulses synced to meter.

### 3. Smartboard Sorters & Class Polls
- **Grade 3 "Save vs Donate vs Spend"**: Drag floating digital coins into the three classroom glass jars.
- **Grade 4 "Car Showroom Comparison"**: Drag feature badges (*Zero Smoke*, *Noisy*, *Rechargeable*, *Runs on Sunlight*) to the correct vehicle.

### 4. Songs and Chants (Titles & Pedagogical Topic Only)
- Grade 3 Chant: *"The Money Timeline Chant"* (Topic: Barter to shells to coins to cards).
- Grade 4 Chant: *"Clean Wheels, Green World"* (Topic: Electric, solar, and saving the polar bear).

## 5. Eight-Lesson Curriculum Outlines (35 Minutes Each)

### Grade 3: "How Much Is It?" (*Global Readings 2*)
- **Lesson 1**: *The Coin Jars* (Unit opener, Save vs Donate, asking "How much is it?").
- **Lesson 2**: *Trade Before Money* (Reading 1 Part 1: Barter, grain, goats, cowrie shells).
- **Lesson 3**: *From Metal Coins to Paper Cash* (Reading 1 Part 2: Lydia stamped coins, China paper money).
- **Lesson 4**: *Plastic and Pixels* (Reading 1 Part 3: Credit cards, computers, timeline check).
- **Lesson 5**: *The Autumn Walk* (Reading 2 Part 1: Phone envy, finding the wet $100 bill).
- **Lesson 6**: *Paige's Big Dilemma* (Reading 2 Part 2: "Money is tight", modals could vs should).
- **Lesson 7**: *Pizza with Dad* (Reading 2 Part 3: The restaurant surprise, story mapping).
- **Lesson 8**: *Smart Shoppers & Phonics Workshop* (Spelling pattern 'ar', problem-solution synthesis).

### Grade 4: "It Keeps Changing" (*Global Readings 3*)
- **Lesson 1**: *The Pink River* (Unit opener, tracking meltwater, climate change big idea).
- **Lesson 2**: *Alone on the Arctic Ice* (Reading 1 Part 1: The fisherman, ice hole, freezing wind).
- **Lesson 3**: *The Ice Begins to Crack* (Reading 1 Part 2: Polar bear arrives, thin ice trap).
- **Lesson 4**: *We Are Not Enemies* (Reading 1 Part 3: Mutual survival, poetry performance, -ng phonics).
- **Lesson 5**: *The Trouble with Gasoline* (Reading 2 Part 1: Crude oil, noise, dirty exhaust smog).
- **Lesson 6**: *Plant Power and Battery Drive* (Reading 2 Part 2: Biofuel and electric vehicles).
- **Lesson 7**: *Sunlight on Wheels* (Reading 2 Part 3: Solar cars, compressed air, comparative adjectives).
- **Lesson 8**: *Saving the Ice Together* (Car comparison matrix, climate action plan for polar bears).
