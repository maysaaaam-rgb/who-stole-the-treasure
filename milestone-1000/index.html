🎆 1,000 XP Ceremony

Play this on the smartboard the moment a pupil reaches 1,000 XP (Level 5, Adventurer). In the platform it also starts by itself for every pupil who reaches Level 5.

🔒 The photo or cartoon avatar is shrunk and kept only in this browser on this computer. It is never uploaded and is not part of the website or GitHub.

🎁 Real-world surprise ideas

  • Class parade: the pupil is "Adventurer of the Week" and leads the line, with a paper crown the class makes.
  • VIP seat for a week: the teacher's special chair, a cushion and their own name card on the desk.
  • Pick the class game: they choose the next game or song for the whole class.
  • Little helper job: "Monster Captain" for a day, with a badge, handing out stickers or choosing who goes next.
  • Gold sticker pack and a monster badge pin to wear on their bag.
  • Message to the parent: a short photo note ("Today [name] reached 1,000 XP!") with the printed certificate.
  • Class applause tunnel: everyone lines up and gives a high five as they walk through.
  • Mystery box: a small box with a pencil, notebook and a handwritten note from you.
