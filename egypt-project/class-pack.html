Mission 1 · Pyramid Builders

📖 Learn

  • The pyramids in Giza are very old.
  • They are tombs for pharaohs.
  • A pyramid has a square base and four triangle sides.

  3. It is bigger than the small pyramid.

How to play Senet

  1. Two players. Each player has 5 pieces. Put them on the first squares, one colour after the other.
  2. Throw the 4 sticks. Count the painted sides: that is how many squares you move (4 white sides = move 5).
  3. Move along the board like a snake: row 1 left to right, row 2 right to left, row 3 left to right.
  4. You cannot land on your own piece. If you land on the other player’s piece, you change places.
  5. The first player with all 5 pieces off the board wins!
🗣️ Game English
  1. It’s my turn. / It’s your turn.
  2. Throw the sticks! One, two, three.
  3. I move three squares.
  4. Change places!
  5. I win! · Good game! Thank you.

Our class rules for Senet: easier than the real game (nobody knows all the old rules today).
