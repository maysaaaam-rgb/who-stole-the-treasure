Marking the writing (accuracy)
MarkWhat it shows
2A full sentence that is clear and uses the right form (past verb, question word, because, so, is + -ing...). Small spelling mistakes are fine.
1The meaning is clear but the grammar has a mistake (for example “I play football yesterday”) or the sentence is not finished.
0Blank, copied from the task only, or not understandable.

These are classroom bands made for this quiz. They match the A1 to A1+ descriptors in a simple way. They are not an official CEFR certificate. The quiz tests English in new situations, not memory of the stories. Use the unit bands to see where each pupil needs support; the speaking lesson next time adds speaking.

After the quiz: what to practise
If a pupil is below A1+ in...Practise
Unit 1has got / have got; present simple with -s (cleans, does); room and machine words (mess, tidy, invent).
Unit 2There is / There are; thankful for; because; festival words (harvest, feast, festival).
Unit 3Past simple with -ed (walked, played); did not + verb; than; was / were; money words (expensive, coins, cashier).
Unit 4Past simple in stories (lifted, arrested, barked); because and so; story words (thunder, sunrise, painting, museum).
