💡 Tips

  • Write a word with the pen and wait: it becomes neat, and spelling mistakes are fixed.
  • 🏫 Classic board: tap a chalk stick on the tray to write in that colour. Pick up the duster and wipe, just like a real board.
  • Draw a circle, line, arrow, square, triangle or star and hold the pen still for a moment: it becomes perfect.
  • Draw a circle around a word: the circle glows and the word is read aloud.
  • ✍️ Challenge and 🏁 Race use today's lesson words (📚). Two children can write at the same time in a race.
  • Page Down / Page Up move between pages. 📂 saves the board for the class and date.
  • Draw a picture and wait: tap 🎨 Make a picture to turn it into a real one.
  • Type / to see all commands. Drag cards to move them, the corner dot to resize.
  • Paste (Ctrl+V) or drag-and-drop any picture or text (e.g. from Gemini) right onto the board.
  • Keys: / commands · Ctrl+Z undo · Delete removes the selected card.
