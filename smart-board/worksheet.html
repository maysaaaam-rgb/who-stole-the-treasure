🪄 Smart Board · Command Card

Type a command in the bar at the bottom of the board, or tap a chip. AI commands need the AI switched on in ⚙️.

✍️ The pen: write a word and wait 2 seconds. It turns into neat letters, and spelling mistakes are fixed and shown in green. Draw a picture and wait: tap 🎨 Make a picture to turn it into a real one. Undo (↶ or Ctrl+Z) always brings things back. Drag cards to move them; drag the blue corner dot to resize.
