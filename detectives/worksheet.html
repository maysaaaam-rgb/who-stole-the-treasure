⚡ THE GOLDEN INTERROGATION RULE (DID & DIDN'T):
When you ask a question with DID, the verb changes back to its BASE form!
❌ Did you went to the park? ➔ ✅ Did you go to the park?
Suspect answers: "Yes, I did." or "No, I didn't."
