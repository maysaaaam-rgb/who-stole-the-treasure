⚠️ THE GOLDEN DETECTIVE RULE:
When you use DID in questions or DIDN'T in negatives, the verb goes back to its base form!
❌ Did you went? ➔ ✅ Did you go?
❌ I didn't ate pizza. ➔ ✅ I didn't eat pizza.
