📋 Phonics Sound Hunters — Complete 20-Point Lesson Plan

1. Lesson Title

Phonics Sound Hunters: The Quest for Long Vowels, Digraphs & Blends

2. Target Age & CEFR Level

Ages 7–9 years old (Grade 2–3) · Beginner / CEFR A1 Level.

3. Lesson Duration

45–60 Minutes (Modular mission-based structure).

4. Learning Objectives

  • Recognize and pronounce Long Vowels: A /eɪ/, E /iː/, I /aɪ/, O /oʊ/, U /uː/.
  • Identify common spelling patterns: a_e, ee, ea, i_e, o_e, oa, ow, u_e, ue, ui.
  • Distinguish long vowel sounds from short vowel contrasts (e.g. cake vs cat, tree vs bed).
  • Recognize and pronounce digraphs: SH /ʃ/, CH /tʃ/.
  • Recognize and pronounce consonant blends: ST, PL, FL, PR, FR.
  • Blend phonemes to decode, build, and spell target phonics vocabulary.

5. Target Phonics & Sounds

Long Vowels: /eɪ/, /iː/, /aɪ/, /oʊ/, /uː/
Digraphs: /ʃ/ (sh), /tʃ/ (ch)
Blends: /st/ (st), /pl/ (pl), /fl/ (fl), /pr/ (pr), /fr/ (fr)

6. Textbook Target Vocabulary

Long A: cake, plane, rain, table, grapes, paint, baby, plate, main, way.
Long E: tree, teeth, sweet, bee, these, green, leaf, eat, teach, pretty (vs bed, hen, nest, mess, red, net, leg, pen).
Long I: Friday, kind, nine, bike, ice, lion, climb, drive, five, line.
Long O: boat, soap, goat, bowl, yellow, toe, hippo, sofa, cold.
Long U: glue, juice, Tuesday, blue, fruit, soup, room, oops, you.
Digraph SH: shells, fish, shoes, wash, shower, shirt, brush, short.
ST & CH: stick, chair, star, check, strawberries, chicken, storm, child.
PL & FL: plant, flap, fly, plum, float, plane, flower, play, plate.
PR & FR: prize, pretty, frog, fruit, friend, from.

7. Materials Needed

Smartboard or projector (running this web app), 4 team sound boards/signs (Long A, E, I, O, U posted around classroom), letter flashcards for physical building, mini whiteboards or scrap paper.

8. Classroom Setup

Students arranged in 4 balanced teams (Lions, Tigers, Foxes, Frogs). Room cleared slightly in the center for movement or clear sightlines to point to vowel posters on 4 walls.

9. Teacher Preparation

1. Open this Phonics Sound Hunters web app in full screen.
2. Test audio toggle.
3. Assign students to the 4 team tables.
4. (Optional) Hide the 7 word hunt cards around the classroom for Mission 8.

10. Step-by-Step Procedure & Exact Scripts

Mission 0 (3m): "Welcome Sound Hunters! Today 5 sound monsters have hidden their secret sounds. Work in your teams to earn stars and defeat the SH Monster and Grand Dragon!"
Mission 1 (5m): "Listen: CAKE. What sound? /eɪ/! Make the Long A gesture!" (Students make 'A' shape).
Mission 2 (6m): "RUN TO THE SOUND! When I say GOAT, point or run to Long O! 3, 2, 1... GO!"
Mission 3 (7m): "Look at the picture: CAKE! How do we spell the sound? a_e!"
Mission 4 (5m): "PHONICS BOMB! If you hear a long vowel, CLAP 👏! If you hear a short vowel, FREEZE 🧊!"
Mission 5 (6m): "The SH Monster says SHHH 🤫! If the word has SH, make the quiet gesture! If not, hands on heads 🙆!"
Mission 6 (7m): "Build the word: S-T-A-R -> STAR! Say it together!"
Mission 7 (6m): "Blend race! PL, FL, PR, or FR? FLOWER starts with FL!"
Mission 8 (5m): "Word hunt! Find PLATE, PRETTY, FRIES, CHAIR, FLOAT, STAND, SHIRT!"
Final Boss (5m): "The Grand Sound Dragon appears! Rapid-fire round to drain its 120 HP!"
Exit Ticket (3m): "Show me your 3 sound badges before you leave!"

11. Exact Student Responses Expected

• "Cake! Long A! /eɪ/! a_e!"
• "Tree! Long E! /iː/! ee!"
• "Shhh! Shell has SH!"
• "Freeze! Bed is short e!"

12. Games & Classroom Rules

• Rule 1: Whisper when thinking, speak loudly together when answering.
• Rule 2: Pointing is safe; no pushing when moving to sound zones.
• Rule 3: Any team shouting over another loses 1 star.

13. Timing for Every Stage

M0: 4m · M1: 5m · M2: 6m · M3: 7m · M4: 5m · M5: 6m · M6: 7m · M7: 6m · M8: 5m · Boss: 5m · Exit: 3m. Total: ~55 mins.

14. Scoring System

Each correct sound match = +1 Star. Defeating SH Monster = +5 Stars. Defeating Boss = +10 Stars. Winning team celebrated at end.

15. Differentiation Strategies

• Emerging learners: Focus on gestures and pointing; partner with peer mentors.
• Advanced learners: Spell the pattern on mini-whiteboard and create another rhyming word (e.g. cake -> bake, lake).

16. Formative Assessment

Teacher uses Mission 4 (Clap/Freeze) and Mission 5 (Quiet/Heads) to observe 100% whole-class listening accuracy in real time.

17. Exit Ticket Check

3 quick checks: 1. cake (Long A), 2. fish (SH), 3. plane (PL + Long A). Record mastery on digital exit ticket.

18. Optional Homework / Reinforcement

"Draw your own Sound Monster! Give it 3 words with Long A or Digraph SH to feed on!"

19. Extra Time Backup Activity

The Human Word Train: 4 students hold letter cards (e.g. S, T, A, R) and scramble themselves across the front of the room. Teams shout instructions to put them in the right order!

20. Offline No-Tech Fallback Version

Write A, E, I, O, U in 4 corners of the board. Call out words with teacher voice and clapping. Use physical textbook pages and printed paper cards for the hunt.
