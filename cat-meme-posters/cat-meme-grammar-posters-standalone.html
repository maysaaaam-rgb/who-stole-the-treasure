Singular Pronouns

1 Boy • 1 Girl • 1 Thing

HE
1 Boy / Male
"He is wearing a shemagh."
He = A handsome gentleman cat!
SHE
1 Girl / Female
"She is a lovely princess."
She = One fabulous girl cat!
IT
1 Object / Thing / Box
"What is the cat inside? IT is a box!"
Use IT for things, places, and toys.

Talking To & Groups

Direct Address • Subjects • Objects

YOU
Looking Right At The Reader
"Yes, YOU! Pay attention!"
I am talking directly to YOU.
THEY
Doing The Action (Subject)
"THEY are planning a secret."
They do the action!
THEM
Receiving The Action (Object)
"Give the treats to THEM!"
The snack goes to them.

Pointing: 1 Thing

Close vs. Far Away

THIS
🟢 1 ITEM + CLOSE
"THIS kitten is in my hand!"
Use THIS for 1 thing that is near to you.
THAT
🔴 1 ITEM + FAR AWAY
"THAT grumpy cat is on the roof!"
Use THAT for 1 thing that is far away.

Pointing: 2+ Things

Plural Close vs. Plural Far Away

THESE
🟢 2+ ITEMS + CLOSE
"THESE kittens are right on my desk!"
Use THESE for many things close to you.
THOSE
🔴 2+ ITEMS + FAR AWAY
"THOSE cats are across the yard!"
Use THOSE for many things far away.
