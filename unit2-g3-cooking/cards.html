🍡 Chuseok Cooking: Choose ONE treat!

Cook it with your family. Take 3 photos and 1 short video. Ask an adult to help!

🍹

Fruit Punch (hwachae)

Easy · no cooking
You need: fruit (apple, pear, strawberries or watermelon), apple juice, a little honey, a big bowl
🧼Wash the fruit.
🔪An adult cuts the fruit.
🥣Add the fruit to a bowl.
🧃Pour the juice.
🍯Add honey and mix.
🍙

Rice Balls (jumeokbap)

Easy · an adult cooks the rice
You need: cooked rice, a little salt, sesame seeds, a little oil
🍚An adult cooks the rice.
🧂Add salt, sesame and oil.
🥄Mix.
🧼Wash your hands.
🤲Roll small balls.
🍡

Songpyeon (rice cakes)

Harder · an adult steams them
You need: rice flour, warm water, honey, sesame seeds (or sugar)
🥄Mix rice flour and warm water.
🤲Roll small balls.
🍯Fill with honey and sesame.
🌙Press into half moons.
♨️An adult steams them.
⚠️ Safety: children never use a knife or the stove alone. Wash your hands first. Check for food allergies.

Dear Parents,

This week in English, Grade 3 learned about Chuseok, the Korean harvest festival, and its treats. They learned cooking words: mix, pour, roll, fill, press, steam, add, cut, and how to tell a recipe with First, Then, Finally.

Home mission: please cook ONE easy treat with your child (fruit punch, rice balls or songpyeon; the recipe card is attached). Your child helps, and an adult does any cutting or cooking on the stove.
