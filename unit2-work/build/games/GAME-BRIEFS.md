# GAME 1 (Grade 3): Chuseok Full Moon Weavers

### 1. Game Concept & Curriculum Alignment
- **Grade & CEFR:** Grade 3 (CEFR A1)
- **Topic:** Nonfiction *Chuseok: Korea's Harvest Festival* (weaving contest, full moon, songpyeon rice cakes, ancestor ceremonies).
- **Target Grammar Formula:** `I am thankful for [noun] because [reason].` & `There is / There are`
- **1-Sentence Pitch:** Weave festive cloth and gather harvest gifts under the full moon while mastering gratitude formulas.
- **Duration Modes:** 15-minute quick sprint (10 core rounds) or 30-minute tournament (30 rounds + teleprompter speeches).

### 2. Whole-Class Smartboard Mechanics & Parallel Desk Action
- **Smartboard Action:** Team champion touches 72px answer cards, spins the loom shuttle, or triggers pronunciation audio.
- **Parallel Desk Action:** All pupils at desks vote with Green/Red cards or 1–4 fingers before the board champion taps. Every correct answer earns +5 XP for their team.
- **Soft-Fail Behavior:** No negative points or buzzer noises! Incorrect taps produce a warm two-tone chime and an encouraging text/voice hint (`"Check who weaves the cloth!"`), allowing immediate retry.

- **Grade & CEFR:** Grade 3 (CEFR A1)
- **Topic:** Fiction *First Harvest* (indoor greenhouse, strange crops, pink potatoes, purple corn, limited water, space suits, helmets, surprise Mars setting).

# GAME 3 (Grade 3): The -st Sound Vault

### 1. Game Concept & Curriculum Alignment
- **Grade & CEFR:** Grade 3 (CEFR A1)
- **Topic:** Phonics final consonant blend `-st` (*fast, last, lost, best, test, harvest, first*, *nest*); minimal pairs vs `-t`, `-s`.
- **Target Phonics Formula:** Sharp /st/ release stopping immediately without Turkish epenthetic vowel (*fast*, NOT *fast-ı*).
- **1-Sentence Pitch:** Crack the secret phonetic vault by identifying, sorting, and pronouncing sharp final -st blends without adding extra vowels.
- **Duration Modes:** 15-minute drill (10 rounds) or 30-minute vault-cracking tournament (30 rounds + chant mode).

### 2. Whole-Class Smartboard Mechanics & Parallel Desk Action
- **Smartboard Action:** Team safe-cracker spins safe dials, slots golden letter keys, or tests word sounds.
- **Parallel Desk Action:** Desk pupils clap once sharply on the final /t/ release: `"FA-ST [clap]! LA-ST [clap]!"`
- **Soft-Fail Behavior:** Vault dial wobbles with a gentle bell sound, reminding pupils: *"Stop the /t/ sharply! No extra sound at the end!"*

### 3. Smartboard Screen ASCII Layout

- **Grade & CEFR:** Grade 4 (CEFR A1+)
- **Topic:** Nonfiction *Two Different Forests* (deciduous broad leaves, leaf drop, 4 seasons vs coniferous evergreen needles, cones, -50°C freezing, hibernation, camouflage).
- **Target Grammar Formula:** `Animals adapt so they can survive.` & `They have [A] instead of [B].`
- **1-Sentence Pitch:** Trek through deciduous and coniferous biomes, matching animal adaptations with *so they can* and *instead of*.

# GAME 5 (Grade 4): The Golden Swallow's Gift

- **Grade & CEFR:** Grade 4 (CEFR A1+)
- **Topic:** Fiction folk tale *A New Friend* (kind Go Ne, injured baby swallow, vet bicycle ride, golden melon fruit, greedy Wen Shu, slingshot, roof repair, reconciliation).
- **Target Grammar Formula:** Narrative irregular past verbs (`built, fell, grew, paid, hit, gave`) & `so they could...`
- **1-Sentence Pitch:** Follow the folk tale of kind Go Ne and greedy Wen Shu, practicing past irregular verbs and story purpose.
- **Duration Modes:** 15-minute quick sprint (10 rounds) or 30-minute story tournament (30 rounds + storytelling teleprompter).

# GAME 6 (Grade 4): The 'ai' Rain Train

### 1. Game Concept & Curriculum Alignment
- **Grade & CEFR:** Grade 4 (CEFR A1+)
- **Topic:** Phonics vowel team `ai` (/eɪ/): *rain, train, mail, tail, nail, sail, paint, wait, main, chain*; contrast with short `/æ/`; author's purpose signals.
- **Target Phonics Formula:** Smooth, prolonged vowel diphthong /eɪ/ without Turkish truncation.
- **1-Sentence Pitch:** Fuel the steam locomotive across phonetic bridges by matching vowel team 'ai' words (/eɪ/) and author's purpose signals.
- **Duration Modes:** 15-minute sprint (10 rounds) or 30-minute grand railway express (30 rounds + live conductor announcements).

### 2. Whole-Class Smartboard Mechanics & Parallel Desk Action
- **Smartboard Action:** Team engineer pulls the steam whistle cord, loads cargo crates stamped with `ai`, and flips railway track switches.
- **Parallel Desk Action:** Entire class pulls imaginary steam whistle cords (`"Choo-choo!"`) and stretches arms wide to demonstrate long `/eɪ/`.

# SUMMARY OF CLASSROOM DEPLOYMENT RULES
1. **Zero Setup Delay:** Every game runs directly inside any standard browser on the teacher's smartboard with no npm, Vite, or login blockers.
2. **Synchronized Engagement:** The pupil at the board is the physical executor for their team, but *every pupil at their desk must signal their answer before the tap occurs*.
3. **Inclusive Scoring:** Soft-fail ensures timid or struggling pupils are celebrated for attempts without penalties or humiliation.
