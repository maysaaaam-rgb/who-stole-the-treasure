# PART 1: GRADE 3 HOMEWORK TASKS (Zero Printing, 10–15 Minutes)
*Designed so an 8–9 year old child can complete them independently or with a parent who does not speak English.*

### Task 1: The "Three Thank You Treasures" Hunt (Speaking & Drawing)
- **Time:** 10–15 minutes
- **Materials:** Student notebook and colored pencils (no printing).
- **Instructions for Pupil:**
  1. Walk around your home. Find 3 things you are truly thankful for (e.g. your bed, a delicious apple, your warm shoes, your school bag).
  2. Draw each item neatly in your English notebook.
  3. Under each drawing, write the sentence: `I am thankful for my [bed/apple/shoes].`
  4. Point to the drawings and say the sentences out loud to your parent: *"Mum/Dad, listen! I am thankful for my bed!"*
- **Parent Check:** Parent signs their initials or draws a star (⭐) at the bottom of the page if the child spoke the 3 English sentences.

---

### Task 2: The Moon & Sun Weather Diary (Observation & Writing)
- **Time:** 10 minutes
- **Materials:** Student notebook and pencil.
- **Instructions for Pupil:**
  1. In the evening, look out the window at the night sky. Can you see the moon?
  2. Draw the shape of the moon you see tonight (full moon 🌕 or crescent moon 🌙).
  3. Write 2 sentences in your notebook using our target words:
     - `There is a moon in the sky.`
     - `I am thankful for the bright moon.`
  4. Teach your parent the word `moon` (/muːn/) and tell them why Korean families celebrate under the full moon at Chuseok.
- **Parent Check:** Parent asks in Turkish: *"Bu ay hangi şekle benziyor?"* and listens to the child answer in English: *"Full moon"* or *"Half moon"*.

---

### Task 3: The Secret Mars Greenhouse Inventor (Creative Thinking & Grammar)
- **Time:** 12–15 minutes
- **Materials:** Student notebook and crayons.
- **Instructions for Pupil:**
  1. Imagine you have a greenhouse dome on the planet Mars!
  2. Invent ONE strange space vegetable (like the pink potatoes or purple corn in our story *First Harvest*). What color is it? (e.g. blue carrots 🥕, polka-dot tomatoes 🍅, striped bananas 🍌).
  3. Draw your strange space vegetable.
  4. Write 2 sentences below your drawing:
     - `There is a [blue carrot] in my greenhouse.`
     - `Check your space suit before you go outside!`
- **Parent Check:** Child shows the drawing to their parent and explains in Turkish why astronauts must wear helmets and space suits on Mars.

---

### Task 4: The -ST Sound Clapping Challenge (Phonics Speaking)
- **Time:** 10 minutes
- **Materials:** Pupil's voice and hands (no paper needed!).
- **Instructions for Pupil:**
  1. Stand in front of your parent or sibling.
  2. Read these 5 magic words aloud, clapping your hands together SHARPLY on the final /t/ sound:
     - `FAST!` *(Clap!)*
     - `LAST!` *(Clap!)*
     - `BEST!` *(Clap!)*
     - `TEST!` *(Clap!)*
     - `HARVEST!` *(Clap!)*
  3. Tell your parent: *"In English, I must stop sharply on the 't' sound. I must not say 'fast-ı'!"*
  4. Say this sentence 3 times as fast as you can without making a mistake:  
     `The fast runner was first, not last!`
- **Parent Check:** Parent claps along and checks that the child stops sharply on the 't'.

# PART 2: GRADE 4 HOMEWORK TASKS (Zero Printing, 10–15 Minutes)

### Task 1: The Kitchen Tree Detective (Contrast Grammar)
- **Time:** 10–15 minutes
- **Materials:** Student notebook and pencil.
- **Instructions for Pupil:**
  1. Look around your home or neighborhood. Find TWO things that can be compared (e.g. a wooden table vs a metal chair; an apple vs a pinecone; a winter coat vs a summer t-shirt).
  2. Write 2 sentences in your notebook using our target contrast phrase `instead of`:
     - `Example 1: Coniferous trees have needles instead of leaves.`
     - `Example 2: In winter, I wear a coat instead of a t-shirt.`
  3. Teach your parent what `instead of` means in Turkish (*"... yerine"*).
- **Parent Check:** Parent asks: *"Instead of ne demekti?"* Child replies: *"-in yerine demektir!"* Parent signs notebook.

---

### Task 2: The Winter Adaptation Reporter (Science & Purpose Grammar)
- **Time:** 12–15 minutes
- **Materials:** Student notebook and colored pencils.
- **Instructions for Pupil:**
  1. Choose ONE forest animal: a **Bear** 🐻 or a **Snowshoe Hare** 🐇.
  2. Draw the animal in its winter habitat (a bear in a cozy den, or a white hare in the snow).
  3. Write 2 sentences explaining how it survives using `so they can` / `so it can`:
     - *If you chose the bear:* `The bear hibernates in a den so it can save energy.`
     - *If you chose the hare:* `The hare turns white so it can hide in the snow.`
  4. Tell your family at dinner how cold coniferous forests get in winter (below -50°C!).
- **Parent Check:** Parent listens to the child read their 2 English sentences aloud.

---

### Task 3: The Golden Swallow Story Retell (Storytelling & Past Verbs)
- **Time:** 10–15 minutes
- **Materials:** Pupil's voice and hands.
- **Instructions for Pupil:**
  1. Sit with your parent, grandparent, or sibling.
  2. Retell the folk tale *A New Friend* in 4 simple English steps (you can translate into Turkish as you go):
     - Step 1: *"A swallow built a nest, and a baby bird fell down."*
     - Step 2: *"Kind Go Ne rode his bicycle and paid the vet."*
     - Step 3: *"The mother bird gave a magical seed that grew into gold!"*
     - Step 4: *"Wen Shu was sorry and they became true friends."*
  3. Explain the moral of the story to your family: *Kindness brings true happiness!*
- **Parent Check:** Family member gives the child a high-five or draws a smiley face in their notebook for retelling the story.

---

### Task 4: The 'AI' Rain Train Rhyme Challenge (Phonics & Speaking)
- **Time:** 10 minutes
- **Materials:** Student notebook and pencil.
- **Instructions for Pupil:**
  1. Write down these 6 vowel team `'ai'` words in your notebook:
     - `RAIN`  |  `TRAIN`  |  `MAIL`  |  `TAIL`  |  `PAINT`  |  `WAIT`
  2. Circle the letters `AI` in each word.
  3. Read the rhyme aloud to your parent with long, smooth train whistle sounds:
     `Wait for the train in the pouring rain!`  
     `The dog wagged its tail with the morning mail!`  
  4. Show your parent that `ai` makes the long English sound `/eɪ/`, just like the letter name A!
- **Parent Check:** Parent listens to make sure the child does not say short *ren* or *tren*, but smooth *ray-een* and *tray-een*.

# PART 3: BILINGUAL PARENT LETTERS (Turkish & English)

## 💌 Grade 3 Family Letter: Unit 2 "Thank You"

### [Türkçe Versiyon — Velilerimiz İçin]
**Değerli 3. Sınıf Velimiz,**

İngilizce dersimizde **Global Readings 2 — Unit 2: "Thank You" (Teşekkür Ederim)** ünitesine başlamış bulunuyoruz! Bu ünitede öğrencilerimiz hem minnettarlık ve teşekkür ifadelerini öğrenecek hem de farklı kültürlerin sonbahar hasat kutlamalarını keşfedecekler.

**Bu Ünitede Neler Öğreniyoruz?**
1. **Okuma Parçaları:** Kore'nin geleneksel sonbahar hasat bayramı olan **Chuseok**'u (dokuma yarışması, ay ışığında dans, songpyeon pirinç keki) ve Mars gezegenindeki gizemli bir sera çiftliğinde geçen **"First Harvest" (İlk Hasat)** hikayesini okuyoruz.
2. **Okuma Becerisi (Reading Skill):** *Olay Yeri ve Zamanı (Understanding the Setting)*. Hikayenin nerede ve ne zaman geçtiğini bularak kahramanların neden uzay giysisi giydiğini keşfediyoruz.
3. **Dilbilgisi:** 
   - `I am thankful for...` (*... için teşekkür ederim / minnettarım*)
   - `There is a [tekil] / There are [çoğul]` (*... var*)
   - `Why? Because...` (*Neden? Çünkü...*)
4. **Ses Bilgisi (Phonics):** Kelime sonundaki `-st` ses birleşimi (*fast, last, lost, best, test, harvest, first*). Türkçedeki ses alışkanlığı nedeniyle çocuklarımız kelime sonuna fazladan ses ekleme eğilimindedir (*fast-ı* gibi). Evde kelimenin sonundaki "t" sesini net ve keskin çıkarmalarını destekleyebilirsiniz.

**Evde Çocuğunuza Nasıl Yardımcı Olabilirsiniz?**
- Çocuğunuza Türkçe olarak: *"Bugün nelere teşekkür etmek istersin?"* diye sorun ve cevabını İngilizce vermesini isteyin: `I am thankful for my family / my toys.`
- Akıllı telefon veya bilgisayar gerektirmeyen haftalık kısa defter ödevlerimizi yaparken çocuğunuzu dinleyin ve defterine bir yıldız (⭐) koyun.
- Çocuğunuzun İngilizceyi eğlenerek ve güvenle konuşması için gösterdiği her çabayı takdir edin.

Desteğiniz ve iş birliğiniz için çok teşekkür ederiz!  
**İngilizce Zümresi — English Adventure Academy**

### [English Version — For School Records]
**Dear Grade 3 Parents and Guardians,**

We are excited to begin **Global Readings 2 — Unit 2: "Thank You"**! In this unit, our pupils explore how people around the world and throughout history express gratitude during the autumn harvest season.

**What We Are Learning in Unit 2:**
1. **Core Readings:** A cultural nonfiction text on **Chuseok: Korea's Harvest Festival** (traditional weaving contests, ancestor ceremonies, full moon circle dances) and an imaginative science fiction story **First Harvest**, where a family celebrates an unusual harvest inside a Martian biosphere dome.
2. **Reading Skill:** *Understanding the Setting* — analyzing WHERE and WHEN a story happens and how the environment dictates clothing and behavior (e.g. space suits on Mars).
3. **Grammar & Sentence Patterns:**
   - `I am thankful for [noun] because [reason].`
   - `There is [singular] / There are [plural].`
   - Asking and answering: `Why? Because...`
4. **Phonics Focus:** Word-final consonant blend `-st` (*fast, last, lost, best, test, harvest, first*), practicing sharp stops without epenthetic vowel insertion.

**How You Can Support at Home:**
- Ask your child at the dinner table: *"What are you thankful for today?"* Encourage them to say: `I am thankful for [food / family / home]!`
- Listen to your child read their short notebook homework assignments and sign with a star (⭐).
- Celebrate every effort your child makes to speak English with confidence!

Thank you for your ongoing partnership!

## 💌 Grade 4 Family Letter: Unit 2 "What Lives Here?"

### [Türkçe Versiyon — Velilerimiz İçin]
**Değerli 4. Sınıf Velimiz,**

4. Sınıf İngilizce dersimizde **Global Readings 3 — Unit 2: "What Lives Here?" (Burada Ne Yaşar?)** ünitesine başladık! Bu ünite, fen bilimleri (CLIL) ile İngilizce dil eğitimini birleştiren zengin ve heyecan verici bir ekoloji ve değerler ünitesidir.

**Bu Ünitede Neler Öğreniyoruz?**
1. **Okuma Parçaları:**
   - **"Two Different Forests" (İki Farklı Orman):** Yaprak döken (*deciduous*) ve dondurucu kuzey ikliminde yaşayan iğne yapraklı (*coniferous*) ormanların farklarını, hayvanların kış uykusunu (*hibernation*) ve kamuflaj uyumlarını öğreniyoruz.
   - **"A New Friend" (Yeni Bir Dost):** Yaralı bir yavru kırlangıca bisikletiyle yardım eden merhametli Go Ne ile açgözlü komşusu Wen Shu'nun geleneksel hikayesi.
2. **Okuma Becerileri (Reading Skills):** 
   - *Bölüm Başlıklarını Anlama (Understanding Headings)*
   - *Yazarın Amacı (Author's Purpose):* Metnin bilgi vermek amacıyla mı (*to inform*) yoksa eğlendirmek amacıyla mı (*to entertain*) yazıldığını ayırt etme.
3. **Dilbilgisi:**
   - `... so they can [verb]` (*... yapabilsinler diye - amaç bildirme*)
   - `... have [A] instead of [B]` (*... yerine ... var - karşılaştırma*)
   - Hikaye geçmiş zaman fiilleri: `built (inşa etti), fell (düştü), grew (büyüdü), paid (ödedi), hit (vurdu), gave (verdi)`.
4. **Ses Bilgisi (Phonics):** `'ai'` harf ikilisinin çıkardığı uzun 'A' sesi (/eɪ/): *rain, train, mail, tail, sail, paint, wait, main*.

**Evde Çocuğunuza Nasıl Yardımcı Olabilirsiniz?**
- Çocuğunuza: *"Kışın ayılar ve tavşanlar nasıl hayatta kalıyor?"* diye sorun. Çocuğunuzun İngilizce olarak: `Bears hibernate so they can save energy` veya `The hare turns white so it can hide` cümlelerini kurmasını teşvik edin.
- Kısa ödevlerimizi yaparken çocuğunuzun İngilizce cümlelerini size sesli okumasını isteyin.
- Merhamet, hayvan sevgisi ve doğayı koruma üzerine çocuğunuzla kısa sohbetler yapın.

Çocuklarımızın gelişimine sunduğunuz değerli katkılar için teşekkür ederiz!  
**İngilizce Zümresi — English Adventure Academy**

### [English Version — For School Records]
**Dear Grade 4 Parents and Guardians,**

We are thrilled to embark on **Global Readings 3 — Unit 2: "What Lives Here?"**! This unit seamlessly blends English language acquisition with natural science (CLIL) and moral education.

**What We Are Learning in Unit 2:**
1. **Core Texts:**
   - An informational science text, **Two Different Forests**, contrasting temperate deciduous forests with cold northern coniferous biomes, exploring seasonal adaptations such as hibernation and winter camouflage.

   - Contrast structures: `Coniferous trees have needles instead of broad leaves.`
   - Irregular past simple narrative verbs: `built, fell, grew, paid, hit, gave`.
4. **Phonics Focus:** Vowel team `'ai'` (/eɪ/) in words like *rain, train, mail, tail, paint, wait*, contrasting with short /æ/.

**How You Can Support at Home:**
- Encourage your child to share one animal adaptation they learned at school (e.g. how the snowshoe hare turns white in winter).
- Listen to your child read their short notebook sentences aloud and sign their weekly work with a star (⭐).
- Discuss the moral of kindness to animals and nature preservation together at home.
