E. Reading: Headings & Author's Purpose 📖 6 marks

Two Different Forests

Deciduous Forests: These forests grow in Europe, North America, and China. They have four seasons. In autumn, the leaves turn orange and brown, and then they fall. Bears eat a lot of food and hibernate in dens all winter.

Coniferous Forests: These forests grow in very cold northern places. Winters are freezing, down to -50°C. Coniferous trees have needles and cones instead of leaves. Animals like hares and lynx adapt with thick white fur so predators cannot see them.
