📐 Target Grammar Formulas:
• Purpose / Adaptation: [Subject] + [Verb] + so they can + [Verb] (Hares turn white so they can hide.)
• Contrast: [A] + instead of + [B] (Coniferous trees have needles instead of leaves.)
• Past Simple: build -> built, fall -> fell, grow -> grew, pay -> paid
