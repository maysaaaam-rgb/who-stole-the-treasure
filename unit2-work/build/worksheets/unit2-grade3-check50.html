E. Reading: Understanding the Setting 📖 6 marks

First Harvest in the Dome

August and Ruth live with their parents on an indoor farm. Outside the glass dome, the ground is dry, red, and rocky. There are no rivers and no fresh air. Inside, green plants grow with bright lights. The family digs up pink potatoes and picks purple corn. Mom bakes a warm pie. Before the children go outside to play, Mom says: "Put on your space suits and helmets. Check your air supply!"
