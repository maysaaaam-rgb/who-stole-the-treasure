# Grade 3 Unit 2: Final Week Teaching Plan (7 × 40-Minute Sessions)
> **Course:** English / Primary ESL & CLIL  
> **Textbook:** *Global Readings 2*, Unit 2: "Thank You" (pp. 24–39)  
> **Class & Level:** Grade 3 (Classes 3A & 3B), CEFR A1 (Ages 8–9)  
> **First Language:** Turkish (SOV sentence patterns, no grammatical gender, no definite article, initial/final cluster sensitivity)  
> **Classroom Environment:** ONE interactive smartboard at the front of the classroom; NO pupil tablets or individual devices. All learners participate simultaneously using mini whiteboards, dual-color voting cards (Green=True/Yes, Red=False/No), finger gestures (1/2/3/4), and team turns.

---

## Weekly Overview & Curriculum Alignment

- **Big Idea:** Why do we celebrate harvests and give thanks?
- **Reading 1 (Nonfiction):** *Chuseok: Korea's Harvest Festival* (pp. 26–29)
- **Reading 2 (Sci-Fi Fiction):** *First Harvest* — The Mars Greenhouse Surprise (pp. 32–37)
- **Reading Skill:** *Understanding the Setting* (Where are they? When is it? How does the place change what they do?)
- **Phonics Target:** Final consonant blend `-st` (*fast, last, lost, best, test, harvest, first*)
- **Grammar & Language Focus:** 
  1. Expressing gratitude: *"I am thankful for [noun]."*
  2. Describing quantity & existence: *"There is a [singular] / There are [plural]."*
  3. Reasoning: *"Why? Because [clause]."*
- **Assessment Target:** 50-mark Progress Check (38 marks written in Session 6; 12 marks speaking in Session 7).

## Session 1: Chuseok Traditions & Reading 1 Consolidation

### Aim
Pupils will describe traditional Chuseok festival activities, match keywords to visual cues, and explain why families celebrate harvests.

### Materials & Tech Setup
- **Smartboard:** Display 4 high-contrast scene cards (Weaving Contest, Full Moon Feast, Songpyeon Rice Cakes, Ancestor Ceremony) and team scoreboard.
- **Pupil Materials:** Mini whiteboards, dry-wipe pens, wiping cloths.
- **Target Vocabulary:** *festival, harvest, contest, weave, cloth, ancestors, feast, moon*.

### Minute-by-Minute Procedure (40 Minutes)

| Time | Phase | Smartboard Display | Teacher & Pupil Actions (Whole-Class Engagement) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **00–05** | **Warm-Up Chant** | Rhythmic chant with clapping icon: *"Harvest, harvest, thank you all! Autumn leaves begin to fall!"* | Teacher models 4-beat clap. Pupils echo chant with physical clapping and actions (reaping, bowing). |
| **05–15** | **Reading 1 Story Recall** | 4 numbered image hotspots from *Chuseok* (pp. 26–29). | Teacher touches Hotspot 1 (King's weaving contest). Pupils write 1 word on mini whiteboards: `WEAVE` or `CONTEST`. Teacher says: *"3, 2, 1, Flash!"* All hold up boards. Repeat for Hotspots 2–4. |
| **15–25** | **True or False Radar** | 5 statements with large GREEN (True) and RED (False) touch buttons. | Statement 1: *"Chuseok is in winter."* Pupils hold up Red cards. Teacher calls a student from Team Alpha to tap RED on smartboard. Chime sounds (+5 XP). |
| **25–35** | **Setting Detective: Earth Autumn** | Slide showing traditional Korean village during full moon. Prompts: *Where? When?* | Pupils write sentence frame on whiteboards: `It is autumn in Korea.` Pairs turn to elbow partner and say: *"Families eat songpyeon."* |
| **35–40** | **Exit Ticket & Cool-Down** | 3 numbered prompt boxes on screen. | Pupils write answers 1, 2, 3 on whiteboard: 1. `harvest` (hasat), 2. `moon`, 3. `songpyeon`. Teacher checks by row as pupils line up. |

### "If You Are Behind" Shortcut
If Reading 1 was not fully read last week: Skip the whiteboard sentence frame (minutes 25–35). Read pages 26–27 aloud chorally in 3-sentence chunks; students use True/False cards directly.

### Exit Ticket Answers
1. What do we celebrate? → `Harvest`  
2. What shines at night? → `Full moon`  
3. What rice cake do they eat? → `Songpyeon`

## Session 2: The Mystery Crops & Reading 2 (First Harvest)

### Aim
Pupils will track the colorful crops in *First Harvest*, identify the family's problem (water shortage), and infer clues about the unusual farm setting.

### Materials & Tech Setup
- **Smartboard:** Interactive greenhouse display showing 5 colorful crops (pink potatoes, purple corn, striped tomatoes, orange peas, purple carrots).
- **Pupil Materials:** A5 paper crop response cards (Red, Purple, Pink, Orange).
- **Target Vocabulary:** *pick, dig, crops, greenhouse, water, worried, feast, bake*.

### Minute-by-Minute Procedure (40 Minutes)

| Time | Phase | Smartboard Display | Teacher & Pupil Actions (Whole-Class Engagement) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **00–05** | **Color Mystery Warm-Up** | 5 odd-colored vegetables with question marks: *"Is corn usually purple?"* | Class votes thumbs up / thumbs down. Teacher asks: *"What is strange about this farm?"* Pupils answer: *"The colors!"* |
| **05–15** | **Choral Reading & Clue Hunt** | Story text excerpts (pp. 32–34) with audio read-aloud buttons. | Class reads chorally behind teacher finger. Teacher pauses at: *"Dad was worried because there was not much water."* Pupils mimic Dad's worried face. |
| **15–25** | **Crop Sorting Workbench** | Drag-and-drop basket game: Match vegetable name to weird color. | Team member comes to board to drag `Pink Potatoes` into Dad's basket. Pupils at desks hold up matching colored paper card. |
| **25–35** | **Problem & Solution Board** | T-Chart: Problem (*Not much water*) vs Solution (*Save water, indoor greenhouse*). | Pupils copy on whiteboards: `There is purple corn.` / `There are pink potatoes.` Teacher checks *is* (singular) vs *are* (plural). |
| **35–40** | **Exit Ticket** | 3 quick-fire questions on board. | 1. Why was Dad worried? (`water`) 2. What color were the potatoes? (`pink`) 3. What will Mom bake? (`apple pie`). |

### "If You Are Behind" Shortcut
Combine Reading 2 pages 32–34 into a 6-sentence teacher read-aloud. Skip the written T-chart; do whiteboard quick-drawing of 1 crop + label.

### Exit Ticket Answers
1. Dad was worried about: `Water`  
2. The potatoes are: `Pink`  
3. Mom wants to bake: `Apple pie`

## Session 3: The Big Mars Reveal & Reading Skill: Understanding the Setting

### Aim
Pupils will uncover the sci-fi surprise setting in *First Harvest* (Mars base), compare the Earth vs Mars settings, and explain how setting affects clothing and survival.

### Materials & Tech Setup
- **Smartboard:** Dual Setting Comparator: Earth Farm (trees, grass, fresh air) vs Mars Base (red dust, indoor dome, space suits, helmets).
- **Pupil Materials:** Mini whiteboards divided with a center line (`EARTH` | `MARS`).
- **Target Vocabulary:** *setting, space suit, helmet, air, Mars, dome, outside, Earth*.

### Minute-by-Minute Procedure (40 Minutes)

| Time | Phase | Smartboard Display | Teacher & Pupil Actions (Whole-Class Engagement) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **00–05** | **Sound Clue Warm-Up** | Audio clip of heavy breathing inside a helmet (`hiss-whoosh`). Visual: Mystery silhouette. | Teacher asks: *"Who wears this? A firefighter? A diver? An astronaut?"* Pupils whisper prediction to partner. |
| **05–15** | **The Surprise Reading (p. 37)** | Page 37 illustration showing Ruth & August in space suits with "MARS 3000" helmets. | Teacher reads Mom's warning: *"Check your space suits before you go outside! Make sure you have plenty of air!"* Dramatic class gasp! Pupils mime putting on heavy helmet and twisting lock: `Click-click!` |
| **15–27** | **Setting Detective Comparator** | 8 object cards: *apple tree, space suit, oxygen tank, green grass, red rocks, river, indoor dome, songpyeon*. | Teacher taps object. Pupils write `E` (Earth) or `M` (Mars) on their mini whiteboards and flash together. Team representative taps correct column on board. |
| **27–35** | **Language Anchor: Why / Because** | Sentence builder formula: `Why do they need space suits? Because there is no air on Mars.` | Whole class repeats with hand motions: `Why?` (shrug shoulders) -> `Because` (tap head). Pupils complete blank on board: `Because there is no ____ (air).` |
| **35–40** | **Exit Ticket** | 3 Setting questions. | Pupils write on whiteboards: 1. Setting of First Harvest? (`Mars`) 2. What do kids wear outside? (`space suits`) 3. What do they need to breathe? (`air`). |

### "If You Are Behind" Shortcut
Focus entirely on the Mars reveal illustration (p. 37). Contrast space helmet vs school clothes in 10 minutes; move directly to the Earth/Mars whiteboard sort.

### Exit Ticket Answers
1. Where is the farm? → `Mars`  
2. What must they check before going outside? → `Space suits / helmets`  
3. What is limited outside? → `Air / Oxygen`

## Session 4: Phonics Final -st & Language Focus (There is / There are / Thankful for)

### Aim
Pupils will pronounce, decode, and spell words with the final consonant blend `-st` without epenthetic vowels, and construct gratitude sentences using *"I am thankful for..."*.

### Materials & Tech Setup
- **Smartboard:** The `-st` Sound Vault (interactive safe that opens with `-st` words) + Gratitude Tree.
- **Pupil Materials:** Mini whiteboards, printed Phonics Drill slip (Worksheet 3 preview).
- **Target Words:** *fast, last, lost, best, test, harvest, first*.
- **Target Grammar:** *There is a... / There are... / I am thankful for...*

### Minute-by-Minute Procedure (40 Minutes)

| Time | Phase | Smartboard Display | Teacher & Pupil Actions (Whole-Class Engagement) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **00–07** | **Phonics Stop: The Final -st Pop** | High-resonance audio tiles: `fa-ST`, `be-ST`, `harve-ST`, `fir-ST`. | Teacher models stopping the breath sharply on /t/. Turkish learners tend to add /ɯ/ (*fast-ı*). Teacher demonstrates the "Desk Tap": tap desk on /st/ burst! Pupils chant: *"Not fast-ı! Just fa-ST!"* |
| **07–17** | **Minimal Pair Showdown** | Word pairs on board: `best / bet`, `fast / fat`, `nest / net`, `lost / lot`. | Teacher says one word. Pupils show 1 finger for Option A, 2 fingers for Option B. A pupil taps the spoken word on the board. |
| **17–27** | **Grammar Gym: There is vs There are** | Picture of Mars Dome: 1 apple pie, 4 pink potatoes, 1 radio, 6 orange peas. | Pupils hold up 1 finger for `There is a...` or 2 fingers for `There are...`. Teacher calls pupil to write `is` or `are` on the board. |
| **27–36** | **The Gratitude Tree** | Golden tree with empty harvest leaves. | Teacher models: *"I am thankful for my family."* Pupils write their own sentence on whiteboards: `I am thankful for [food / water / friends / my mom].` 4 pupils read aloud to the class. |
| **36–40** | **Exit Ticket** | 3 phonics & grammar check items. | 1. Spell: `b-e-s-t` 2. Choose: `There (is/are) four potatoes.` (`are`) 3. Complete: `I am thankful ___ my dog.` (`for`). |

### "If You Are Behind" Shortcut
Do 5 minutes on `-st` sound drilling and jump directly to `There is / There are` whiteboard practice. Keep Gratitude Tree to 1 oral choral sentence.

### Exit Ticket Answers
1. Word ending in -st: `best / fast / first`  
2. There `are` four potatoes.  
3. I am thankful `for`...

## Session 5: Whole-Class Smartboard Review Arcade

### Aim
Pupils will review all Unit 2 vocabulary, setting details, phonics, and grammar in a high-energy team tournament using Smartboard Game 1 / Game 2.

### Materials & Tech Setup
- **Smartboard:** Unit 2 Classroom Game Kit running on the browser (Teams Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Delta).
- **Pupil Materials:** Numbered answer cards (1, 2, 3, 4) for every child at their desk.
- **Rules:** One child per team comes to touch the board; ALL students at desks answer simultaneously with cards before the board is tapped.

### Minute-by-Minute Procedure (40 Minutes)

| Time | Phase | Smartboard Display | Teacher & Pupil Actions (Whole-Class Engagement) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **00–05** | **Game Setup & Rules** | Game Title: *Harvest Moon & Mars Rover Battle*. 4 Team columns on scoreboard. | Teacher explains: *"No shouting! Cards up first, then board player taps! 5 XP per correct answer!"* |
| **05–17** | **Round 1: Vocabulary & Setting Radar** | 10 rapid-fire multiple choice questions covering Chuseok and Mars greenhouse. | Prompt: *"Where do Ruth and August live? 1. Moon, 2. Mars, 3. Earth, 4. Sun."* Desks: All hold up Card 2. Board player from Team Alpha taps '2'. Success chime! (+5 XP). |
| **17–28** | **Round 2: The -st Phonics Vault** | Unscramble tiles: `f - a - s - t`, `h - a - r - v - e - s - t`. | Team Beta board player arranges tiles on screen. Desks write completed word on whiteboards. Soft-fail enabled: friendly audio hint if a letter slips. |
| **28–36** | **Round 3: Teleprompter Pitch (Speaking)** | 3-sentence karaoke teleprompter: 1. Intro, 2. Harvest tradition, 3. Gratitude. | Team champions come to microphone. Class chants rhythm. Teleprompter highlights words in sync with student speech (+20 Bonus XP). |
| **36–40** | **Victory Fanfare & Debrief** | Final scoreboard with animated medals and fireworks. | Teacher awards star stamps. Quick class review of the most missed question during the game. |

### "If You Are Behind" Shortcut
Run the 15-minute express version of the game kit (10 rounds total). Use the remaining 25 minutes to do targeted re-teaching of Setting and `-st` spelling.

### Exit Ticket Answers
Scoreboard points recorded directly in teacher's gradebook.

## What Pupils Can Do at the End of Unit 2 (Can-Do Summary)

1. **Listening:** Identify harvest celebration foods, family members, festival activities, and unusual crop colors from spoken descriptions.
2. **Reading Comprehension:** Recognize the setting (time, place, environment) in both realistic historical texts (Chuseok) and fictional stories (Mars greenhouse), and identify how setting affects daily life.
3. **Phonics:** Pronounce, distinguish, and write words ending in the final consonant blend `-st` (*fast, last, lost, best, harvest, first*) without vowel epenthesis.
4. **Grammar & Sentence Building:** Use *There is / There are* accurately for singular and plural nouns, answer *Why* questions using *Because*, and express gratitude using *I am thankful for...*.
5. **Speaking:** Deliver 3 coherent connected sentences describing a festival or harvest scene using clear pronunciation and scaffolded sentence frames.
