# Lesson 1: See, Think, Wonder: Biomes & Mysterious Forests
- **Pages:** 24–25 (Unit Big Idea Opener)
- **Aim:** Pupils will examine forest biomes, activate prior knowledge about animal habitats, and use the question frame `What lives in the forest?`.
- **Duration:** 40 Minutes

### 1. 5-Minute Warm-Up (00:00–05:00)
- **Animal Sound & Habitat Match:** Teacher plays ambient nature sounds on the smartboard (chirping woodland birds, howling wind, cracking twigs).
- T asks: *"Close your eyes. Where are we? Are we in a city supermarket or a deep forest?"* Class: *"A forest!"* T: *"Yes! Today we begin Unit 2: What Lives Here? We are nature explorers!"*

### 2. Timed Lesson Steps (40 Minutes Total)
- **05:00–12:00 (7 min) | See-Think-Wonder Routine (Pages 24–25):**
  - Display page 24 photo of a lush temperate forest with sunbeams piercing through tall canopies.
  - Step 1 (SEE): *"Name 3 things you see."* -> Trees, green moss, leaves, shadows.
  - Step 2 (THINK): *"What do you think lives beneath those tall trees?"* -> Bears, deer, birds, insects.
  - Step 3 (WONDER): *"What do you wonder about animals when winter comes and snow covers the ground?"*
- **12:00–22:00 (10 min) | Academic Vocabulary Anchor: Biome & Habitat:**
  - Introduce `biome` (a large ecological community with specific climate and wildlife) and `habitat` (a creature's home).
  - Turkish anchor: Connect *biome* to *biyom / büyük yaşam alanı* and *habitat* to *doğal yaşam alanı*.
  - Board sentence frame: `[Animals] live in the [biome] because [reason].`
  - Model: *"Deer live in the forest because there are lots of plants to eat."*
- **22:00–32:00 (10 min) | Differentiated Habitat Workbench (Mini-Whiteboards):**
  - **Support Task:** Match animals to their habitat pictures: Bear -> Forest Cave; Camel -> Desert; Fish -> Ocean.
  - **Core Task:** Write two sentences using the frame: `A bear lives in the forest. It needs food and shelter.`
  - **Stretch Task:** Write a prediction about how forest animals survive the freezing winter.
- **32:00–37:00 (5 min) | Forest Creature Quick Poll:**
  - Smartboard interactive voting: *"Which forest animal is the most fascinating: Wolf, Bear, or Snowshoe Hare?"* Pupils raise fingers (1, 2, or 3).
- **37:00–40:00 (3 min) | Exit Ticket & Wrap-Up.**

### 4. Classroom Language
- **Teacher Says:** *"Look at page 24. What biome do you see? It is a green forest biome. What animals live here?"*
- **Pupils Say:** *"Bears live here." / "Birds make nests in the trees." / "Animals need food and water."*

### 5. Exit Ticket (3 Questions)
1. *What word means a large ecological area with special plants and climate?* -> **Answer:** Biome
2. *Name one animal that lives in a forest biome.* -> **Answer:** Bear / Deer / Hare / Wolf / Owl
3. *Complete: Birds build nests in the _____.* -> **Answer:** trees

### 6. Common Mistakes for Turkish Learners
- **Omission of plural -s:** Saying *"Bear live in forest"*. Fix: Drill adding plural /s/ or indefinite article: *"A bear lives..."* or *"Bears live..."*.

### 7. Homework Option
- Draw a forest animal in your notebook and write: `A [bear/deer] lives in the forest.`

# Lesson 2: Reading 1: Deciduous Forests & Seasonal Changes
- **Pages:** 26–27
- **Aim:** Pupils will read about deciduous forests, explain why trees drop leaves in autumn, and use the purpose connector `so they can`.
- **Duration:** 40 Minutes

### 1. 5-Minute Warm-Up (00:00–05:00)
- **Four Seasons Spin:** Teacher shows a 4-season wheel on smartboard (🌸 Spring, ☀️ Summer, 🍂 Autumn, ❄️ Winter).
- Class chants: *"Spring grows! Summer glows! Autumn drops! Winter stops!"*

### 2. Timed Lesson Steps
- **05:00–12:00 (7 min) | Vocabulary Ignition (Pages 26–27):**
  - Target words: `deciduous`, `broad leaves`, `drop`, `season`.
  - Turkish breakdown: *Deciduous* = *yaprak döken*. Teach mnemonic: *Deciduous = leaves DECIDE to fall!*
  - TPR Action: Arms spread wide like flat broad leaves; fingers flutter downward to simulate falling leaves.
- **12:00–22:00 (10 min) | Close Reading: The Deciduous Forest:**
  - Shared reading lines 1–16 of *Two Different Forests*.
  - Key facts to extract:
    1. Deciduous forests have 4 distinct seasons.
    2. Leaves are broad and flat to catch summer sunlight.
    3. In autumn, leaves change color (red, orange, yellow) and fall to the ground.
    4. Why? To save water and energy during freezing winter!
- **22:00–32:00 (10 min) | Grammar Focus: `so they can` (Adaptation & Purpose):**
  - Teacher presents formula: `[Action] + so they can + [Purpose]`.
  - Board examples:
    - *Trees drop leaves so they can save water.*
    - *Birds fly south so they can find food.*
  - **Support Task:** Connect sentence halves with lines on worksheet.
  - **Core Task:** Fill in the blank with `so they can`: `Trees drop leaves _____ save energy.`
  - **Stretch Task:** Write an original sentence about an animal adaptation using `so they can`.
- **32:00–37:00 (5 min) | Smartboard Leaf Fall Challenge:**
  - Interactive smartboard game: Pupils touch falling leaves that contain correct deciduous facts (+5 XP).
- **37:00–40:00 (3 min) | Exit Ticket & Wrap-Up.**

### 3. Board Plan
```text
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  DECIDUOUS FORESTS (Yaprak Döken Ormanlar):     GRAMMAR: PURPOSE CONNECTOR        |
|  - 4 seasons: Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter    [Action] + SO THEY CAN + [Verb]   |
|  - Leaves: Broad, flat, green -> red & yellow   - Trees drop leaves so they can   |
|  - Winter: Trees are bare to save water           save water.                     |
|                                                 - Birds fly south so they can     |
|                                                   stay warm.                      |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 4. Classroom Language
- **Teacher Says:** *"Look at the orange trees on page 26. Why do leaves fall? Trees drop leaves so they can save water. Repeat: so they can!"*
- **Pupils Say:** *"So they can save water!" / "Deciduous trees have four seasons."*

### 5. Exit Ticket (3 Questions)
1. *How many seasons does a deciduous forest have?* -> **Answer:** Four
2. *What do deciduous trees do in autumn?* -> **Answer:** Drop their leaves / Change color
3. *Complete: Trees drop leaves so they _____ save water.* -> **Answer:** can

### 6. Common Mistakes for Turkish Learners
- **Confusing 'so that' with 'because':** Turkish learners mix up *çünkü* (reason) and *böylece / -ebilmek için* (purpose). Anchor: *Because* looks backward at a cause; *So they can* looks forward at a goal.

### 7. Homework Option
- Complete Worksheet 1, Exercise A (Deciduous matching).

# Lesson 3: Words in Context: Ecology & Adaptations
- **Pages:** 27–28
- **Aim:** Pupils will master animal survival vocabulary (*hibernate, survive, adapt, den, coat*) and describe how animals prepare for winter.
- **Duration:** 40 Minutes

### 1. 5-Minute Warm-Up (00:00–05:00)
- **Winter Sleep Freeze:** Teacher announces: *"Summer is here! Hunt for berries!"* Pupils pretend to pick food. Teacher calls: *"Winter blizzard! -10 degrees!"* Pupils curl into sleeping balls. Teacher introduces: *"That deep winter sleep is called HIBERNATION!"*

### 2. Timed Lesson Steps
- **05:00–12:00 (7 min) | Vocabulary Deep Dive (Page 27):**
  - Target words: `hibernate`, `survive`, `adapt`, `den`.
  - Turkish links:
    - *hibernate:* kış uykusuna yatmak.
    - *survive:* hayatta kalmak.
    - *adapt:* uyum sağlamak / adapte olmak.
- **12:00–22:00 (10 min) | Close Reading: Winter Strategies:**
  - Read Section: *Surviving the Cold Winter*.
  - Compare two animal survival strategies:
    - Strategy A (Deep Sleep): Bears eat lots of food in autumn, then hibernate in a den all winter.
    - Strategy B (Camouflage Coat): Deer grow thick brown fur; hares turn white.
  - Ask: *"Why do bears hibernate?"* -> *"So they can survive with no food in winter!"*
- **22:00–32:00 (10 min) | Differentiated Practice Workbench:**
  - **Support Task:** Picture-to-word match: Bear sleeping -> Hibernate; Hare turning white -> Adapt; Shield -> Survive.
  - **Core Task:** Fill in the blanks: `Animals must __________ to the cold. Some animals __________ in cozy dens so they can __________.'`
  - **Stretch Task:** Write 3 sentences explaining what would happen to a bear if it did NOT hibernate.
- **32:00–37:00 (5 min) | Animal Strategy Sorter (Smartboard):**
  - Pupils drag animals to the correct winter survival category: *Hibernate* (Bear, Chipmunk, Frog) vs *Stay Active & Camouflage* (Deer, Snowshoe Hare, Wolf).
- **37:00–40:00 (3 min) | Exit Ticket & Wrap-Up.**

|  - survive = hayatta kalmak          (Bears, groundhogs)                          |

### 4. Classroom Language
- **Teacher Says:** *"Look at the sleeping bear in its den. It doesn't eat for months! It hibernates so it can survive."*
- **Pupils Say:** *"Bears hibernate in winter." / "Animals adapt to the cold weather."*

### 5. Exit Ticket (3 Questions)
1. *What word means sleeping through the winter to save energy?* -> **Answer:** Hibernate
2. *Where do bears sleep during winter?* -> **Answer:** In a den
3. *What does 'survive' mean?* -> **Answer:** To stay alive

### 6. Common Mistakes for Turkish Learners
- **Pronouncing 'hibernate':** Turkish pupils often pronounce the silent 'e' at the end (*hi-ber-na-te*). Model silent final 'e': *"HIGH-ber-nayt"*.

### 7. Homework Option
- Write two animals that hibernate and two animals that stay awake in winter.

# Lesson 4: Comprehension: Coniferous Forests & Winter Camouflage
- **Pages:** 28–29
- **Aim:** Pupils will contrast deciduous and coniferous forests, master the contrast formula `have [A] instead of [B]`, and explain how the snowshoe hare survives.
- **Duration:** 40 Minutes

### 1. 5-Minute Warm-Up (00:00–05:00)
- **Pinecone vs Apple Touch:** Teacher shows a real pinecone and an apple (or smartboard photos).
- T asks: *"Which one grows on a Christmas pine tree? Which tree stays green in heavy snow?"* Class: *"The pine tree!"*

### 2. Timed Lesson Steps
- **05:00–12:00 (7 min) | Vocabulary & Concept: Coniferous Forests (Pages 28–29):**
  - Target words: `coniferous`, `evergreen`, `needles`, `cones`.
  - Etymology clue: *Coniferous* = cone-bearing (kozalak taşıyan).
  - Physical contrast: Broad leaves vs Sharp needles.
- **12:00–22:00 (10 min) | Close Reading & Climate Comparison:**
  - Read lines 20–35 on page 28.
  - Temperature shock: Coniferous forests in the north can reach **minus 50 degrees Celsius (-50°C)**!
  - Why needles? Needles are narrow with a waxy coating, losing very little water in freezing winds.
  - Cones: Protect delicate seeds like wooden armor.
- **22:00–32:00 (10 min) | Grammar Focus: `instead of` (Contrast):**
  - Formula: `Subject + have + [Noun A] + instead of + [Noun B]`.
  - Board models:
    - *Coniferous trees have needles instead of broad leaves.*
    - *They have cones instead of flowers or fruit.*
  - **Support Task:** Complete gap-fill: `Coniferous trees have needles instead _____ leaves.`
  - **Core Task:** Rewrite two contrast sentences using `instead of`.
  - **Stretch Task:** Write about the snowshoe hare using both grammar formulas: `The hare has white fur instead of brown fur so it can hide in the snow.`
- **32:00–37:00 (5 min) | Biome Explorer Arcade Game:**
  - Launch `unit2-grade4-game-1.json` on smartboard for 5 quick team biome rounds.
- **37:00–40:00 (3 min) | Exit Ticket & Wrap-Up.**

### 3. Board Plan
```text
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| DATE: Unit 2, Lesson 4      CONIFEROUS FORESTS & CONTRASTS      PAGES: 28-29      |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  CONIFEROUS FORESTS (İğne Yapraklılar):         GRAMMAR: INSTEAD OF               |
|  - Temperature: Reaches below -50°C             [Noun A] + INSTEAD OF + [Noun B]  |
|  - Trees: Pines, firs (Evergreen = always green)- Needles INSTEAD OF leaves       |
|  - Seeds: Protected inside hard woody CONES     - Cones INSTEAD OF fruit          |
|                                                                                   |
|  TURKISH NOTE: "instead of" = "... yerine"                                        |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 4. Classroom Language
- **Teacher Says:** *"Look at the pine tree. Does it drop its needles? No! It is evergreen. It has needles instead of broad leaves."*
- **Pupils Say:** *"Needles instead of leaves!" / "It stays green all year." / "It is minus fifty degrees!"*

### 5. Exit Ticket (3 Questions)
1. *What do coniferous trees have instead of broad leaves?* -> **Answer:** Needles
2. *What do coniferous trees have instead of flowers or fruit?* -> **Answer:** Cones
3. *Complete: Coniferous trees have cones instead _____ flowers.* -> **Answer:** of

### 6. Common Mistakes for Turkish Learners
- **Word order with 'instead of':** Turkish puts *yerine* at the end of the phrase (*yaprak yerine iğne*). English places *instead of* between the two nouns: *needles INSTEAD OF leaves*.

### 7. Homework Option
- Complete Worksheet 2, Section A (Biome Venn diagram).

# Lesson 5: Reading Skill: Understanding the Author's Purpose
- **Pages:** 30–31
- **Aim:** Pupils will learn the 3 author purposes (to INFORM, to ENTERTAIN, to PERSUADE) using the P.I.E. acronym and identify section headings.
- **Duration:** 40 Minutes

### 1. 5-Minute Warm-Up (00:00–05:00)
- **The P.I.E. Chef:** Teacher draws a giant slice of pie on the board:
  - **P** = Persuade (ikna etmek - e.g. buy this candy!)
  - **I** = Inform (bilgilendirmek - e.g. science facts about forests!)
  - **E** = Entertain (eğlendirmek - e.g. funny story or folk tale!)
  - Pupils chant: *"P - I - E! What is the purpose? Let me see!"*

### 2. Timed Lesson Steps
- **05:00–12:00 (7 min) | Teaching the Skill (Pages 30–31):**
  - Review *Two Different Forests*. T asks: *"Did the author write this to make us laugh or to teach us science facts?"* Class: *"To teach us facts!"* T: *"Yes! The author's purpose is TO INFORM!"*
  - Review section headings: Bold titles above paragraphs tell the reader what facts will follow.
- **12:00–22:00 (10 min) | Heading Detective Challenge:**
  - Smartboard displays 4 mystery facts from the text. Pupils must name the correct heading:
    - Fact 1: *"Winter temperatures drop below -50°C."* -> Heading: *Coniferous Forests*
    - Fact 2: *"Leaves change to red and yellow in autumn."* -> Heading: *Deciduous Forests*
    - Fact 3: *"Bears sleep in cozy dens."* -> Heading: *Surviving the Cold Winter*
- **22:00–32:00 (10 min) | Differentiated Practice Workbench:**
  - **Support Task:** Circle: Science book -> To Inform; Comic book -> To Entertain.
  - **Core Task:** Complete sentence frames: `The author wrote 'Two Different Forests' to inform us about __________. The heading tells us __________.`
  - **Stretch Task:** Write a 2-sentence paragraph with a heading: Heading: *My Winter Coat*; Text: *I wear a heavy coat so I can stay warm in the snow.*
- **32:00–37:00 (5 min) | Smartboard Purpose Sorter:**
  - Teams classify 6 book covers into *To Inform* vs *To Entertain*.
- **37:00–40:00 (3 min) | Exit Ticket & Wrap-Up.**

# Lesson 6: Reading 2: A New Friend (The Folk Tale)
- **Pages:** 32–35
- **Aim:** Pupils will read the Korean folk tale *A New Friend*, contrast kind Go Ne with greedy Wen Shu, and identify past irregular verbs (`built, fell, grew, paid, hit, gave`).
- **Duration:** 40 Minutes

### 1. 5-Minute Warm-Up (00:00–05:00)
- **Kindness vs Greed Meter:** Teacher displays two emojis: 😊 (Kind Heart) vs 😈 (Greedy Heart).
- T asks: *"If you find an injured little bird, what do you do? Do you help it or hurt it?"* Class: *"Help it!"*

### 2. Timed Lesson Steps
- **05:00–14:00 (9 min) | Shared Reading Pages 32–33:**
  - Introduce characters: Kind neighbor Go Ne and greedy neighbor Wen Shu.
  - Story Event 1: A swallow built a nest under Go Ne's roof. One day, a baby bird fell.
  - Story Event 2: Go Ne put the bird on his bicycle, rode to the vet, and looked after it.
  - Story Event 3: In spring, the swallow brought back a magical golden seed. It grew into golden fruit!
- **14:00–24:00 (10 min) | Close Reading Pages 34–35:**
  - Story Event 4: Wen Shu was jealous. He took a slingshot and hit a baby bird!
  - Story Event 5: Wen Shu planted a seed, but monsters and weeds came out!
  - Grammar spotlight: Irregular past verbs: `built`, `fell`, `grew`, `paid`, `hit`, `gave`.
- **24:00–34:00 (10 min) | Differentiated Narrative Workbench:**
  - **Support Task:** Match present and past verbs: build -> built; fall -> fell; pay -> paid; give -> gave.
  - **Core Task:** Sequence the 5 story events in chronological order on the worksheet.
  - **Stretch Task:** Write why Go Ne received gold while Wen Shu received weeds: `Go Ne was kind, so he was rewarded. Wen Shu was greedy, so he learned a hard lesson.`
- **32:00–37:00 (3 min) | Folk Tale Morals Discussion:**
  - T asks: *"What is the author's purpose of this story?"* -> *"To entertain and teach us that kindness is best!"*
- **37:00–40:00 (3 min) | Exit Ticket & Wrap-Up.**

# Lesson 7: Skill Practice & Speaking: Remorse, Kindness, and Spelling Pattern 'ai'
- **Pages:** 36–38
- **Aim:** Pupils will analyze story resolution (Wen Shu's remorse & roof repair), master the vowel team `ai` (/eɪ/) phonics, and deliver a 3-sentence oral summary.
- **Duration:** 40 Minutes

### 1. 5-Minute Warm-Up (00:00–05:00)
- **Train Whistle Choo-Choo:** Teacher mimics a steam train: *"Choo-choo! All aboard the 'ai' train! Say: tr-AI-n!"* Class echoes with arms pumping: *"TRAIN! RAIN! SAIL!"*

### 2. Timed Lesson Steps
- **05:00–12:00 (7 min) | Story Resolution & Moral (Pages 36–37):**
  - Read lines 40–50. Wen Shu repents: *"I am so sorry I hurt the bird."*
  - He leaves a generous gift so they can fix Go Ne's roof together.
  - Go Ne forgives him: They become true friends!
- **12:00–22:00 (10 min) | Phonics Workshop: Vowel Team 'ai' (/eɪ/) (Page 38):**
  - Write words on board: `rain`, `train`, `mail`, `tail`, `sail`, `paint`, `wait`, `paid`, `main`.
  - Phonetic rule: When two vowels go walking, the first one does the talking! 'A' and 'I' together say the long /eɪ/ sound.
  - Minimal pair comparison vs short /æ/:
    - `pan` (tava) vs `pain` (ağrı/acı)
    - `ran` (koştu) vs `rain` (yağmur)
    - `man` (adam) vs `main` (ana yol)
  - Desk pair drill with whiteboard flashcards.
- **22:00–32:00 (10 min) | Differentiated Speaking Teleprompter:**
  - Smartboard teleprompter frame:
    - *Sentence 1 (Scaffold):* `A swallow fell from its high nest.`
    - *Sentence 2 (Formula 1):* `Kind Go Ne helped it and paid the animal doctor.`
    - *Sentence 3 (Formula 2):* `Wen Shu left a gift so they could fix the roof together.`
  - **Support Task:** Recite Sentence 1 and 2 in pairs.
  - **Core Task:** Recite all 3 sentences with expression and correct past verbs.
  - **Stretch Task:** Add a fourth sentence expressing the moral: `They learned that kindness is better than greed.`
- **32:00–37:00 (5 min) | The 'ai' Rain Train Arcade Game:**
  - Launch `unit2-grade4-game-3.json` on smartboard for 5 quick team railway rounds.
- **37:00–40:00 (3 min) | Exit Ticket & Wrap-Up.**

# Lesson 8: Think Together: Ecological Connections & Reading Journal
- **Pages:** 39–40
- **Aim:** Pupils will synthesize the unit's themes (animal adaptations and human kindness to nature) and write their final Unit 2 Reading Journal entry.
- **Duration:** 40 Minutes

### 1. 5-Minute Warm-Up (00:00–05:00)
- **Nature Guardians Oath:** Class stands. Teacher recites: *"We are friends of the forest! We protect the trees, we care for the birds, and we respect all biomes!"* Class raises right hand: *"We protect nature!"*

### 2. Timed Lesson Steps
- **05:00–12:00 (7 min) | Unit 2 Synthesis Discussion (Pages 39–40):**
  - T asks: *"How do both readings connect to animals and nature?"*
  - Synthesis points:
    1. In *Two Different Forests*, animals adapt so they can survive in the wild.
    2. In *A New Friend*, humans must protect and help animals in their habitats.
    3. Both texts teach us respect for living things.
- **12:00–22:00 (10 min) | Grammar Tournament (Whole Class Relay):**
  - Divide class into 4 teams. Board displays 4 challenge sentences:
    - Team 1: `Coniferous trees have needles instead of __________.` (broad leaves)
    - Team 2: `Animals hibernate so they can __________.` (survive / save energy)

### 5. Exit Ticket (3 Questions)
1. *Why do coniferous trees have needles instead of broad leaves?* -> **Answer:** To survive the freezing cold / save water
2. *What is one irregular past verb from the story?* -> **Answer:** built / fell / grew / paid / hit / gave
3. *What is the author's purpose in a folk tale?* -> **Answer:** To entertain (and teach a lesson)

### 6. Common Mistakes for Turkish Learners
- **Confusing 'instead of' with 'because of':** Re-drill that *instead of* replaces something, while *because of* gives a reason.
