# GRADE 4 UNIT 2 TEACHING IDEAS & ACTIVITIES
**English Adventure Academy — Global Readings 3: Unit 2 "What Lives Here?"**  
*Audience:* Turkish Primary Learners (CEFR A1+, Grade 4)  
*Setup:* Exactly ONE Interactive Smartboard + Low-Cost Classroom Materials  

---

## 🗣️ Category 1: Speaking Games

### Idea 1: The Forest Biome Contrast Battle (Instead Of Relay)
- **Time:** 12 minutes
- **Prep:** Divide the board into two columns: `DECIDUOUS` and `CONIFEROUS`.
- **Materials:** Whiteboard markers, smartboard timer.
- **Target Words:** *deciduous, coniferous, needles, leaves, cones, fruit, instead of*.
- **Steps:**
  1. Two teams face the board. Team Deciduous holds a leafy branch (or picture); Team Coniferous holds a pine sprig.
  2. Teacher announces a category (e.g. *Leaves*, *Seeds*, *Winter Appearance*).
  3. Team Coniferous speaks first using the contrast formula: `We have needles instead of broad leaves!`
  4. Team Deciduous responds: `We have broad leaves instead of needles!`
  5. Teacher calls *Seeds*: Team Coniferous calls: `We have hard cones instead of soft fruit!`
  6. The fastest team with correct pronunciation and grammar earns +5 XP.
- **Differentiation:**
  - *Support:* Provide the sentence frame with word bank tiles on the smartboard.
  - *Stretch:* Pupils add a reason: `We have needles instead of leaves so we don't lose water in the winter.`
- **Evidence of Learning:** Mastery of the contrast phrase `instead of` and correct noun placement.

### Idea 2: The Adaptation Interview Show
- **Time:** 15 minutes
- **Prep:** 4 animal role badges: *Snowshoe Hare*, *Grizzly Bear*, *White-Tailed Deer*, *Barn Swallow*.
- **Materials:** A rolled-up paper "microphone".
- **Target Words:** *adapt, survive, hibernate, fur, white, den, so they can*.
- **Steps:**
  1. Pupil A plays the "Wildlife TV Host" with the paper microphone; Pupil B plays an adapted animal.
  2. Host asks: *"Hello Mr. Snowshoe Hare! Winter is freezing cold! How do you survive?"*
  3. Animal responds: *"I turn pure white so I can hide from wolves in the deep snow!"*
  4. Host interviews the Bear: *"Why do you eat so much in autumn?"*
  5. Bear responds: *"I eat berries so I can hibernate in my warm den all winter!"*
  6. Swap roles in pairs across the classroom.
- **Differentiation:**
  - *Support:* Pupil reads from an interview prompt card.
  - *Stretch:* Host asks a follow-up question: *"What do you eat when you wake up in spring?"*
- **Evidence of Learning:** Fluent oral execution of the target purpose formula `so I/they can [verb]`.

### Idea 3: The Author's Purpose Interrogation (P.I.E. Courtroom)
- **Time:** 12 minutes
- **Prep:** Prepare 3 large paddle cards: `INFORM (I)`, `ENTERTAIN (E)`, `PERSUADE (P)`.
- **Materials:** Paddles or mini-whiteboards, smartboard displaying mystery text snippets.
- **Target Words:** *author's purpose, inform, entertain, persuade, facts, story, heading*.
- **Steps:**
  1. Smartboard displays an excerpt (e.g. *"Coniferous forests can reach minus 50 degrees Celsius."*).
  2. The classroom "Judge" taps their gavel (a ruler on the desk) and calls: *"What is the author's purpose? 3-2-1 SHOW!"*
  3. All pupils raise their paddle or write `INFORM`, `ENTERTAIN`, or `PERSUADE` on their whiteboard.
  4. Teacher calls on a random pupil to defend their choice: *"Why is it INFORM?"* Pupil: *"Because it gives real science facts with numbers, not a made-up story!"*
- **Differentiation:**
  - *Support:* Pupils check clue words: *facts/numbers = Inform; character/dialogue = Entertain*.
  - *Stretch:* Pupil explains the author's intended effect on the reader.
- **Evidence of Learning:** Metacognitive reading comprehension skill (*Author's Purpose*).

## 🎭 Category 2: Drama & Role-Play

### Idea 4: The Tale of the Two Neighbors (Courtroom of Kindness)
- **Time:** 15 minutes
- **Prep:** 4 pupil actors: *Kind Go Ne*, *Greedy Wen Shu*, *The Vet*, *The Golden Swallow*.
- **Materials:** A toy bird (or paper origami swallow), toy bicycle bell, a bag of golden plastic tokens.
- **Target Words:** *swallow, nest, fell, vet, bicycle, paid, slingshot, hit, gave, remorse, friends*.
- **Steps:**
  1. Act 1: The Swallow nests under Go Ne's roof. The baby bird falls (*"Cheep, cheep! Help!"*).
  2. Act 2: Go Ne places the bird safely in a basket, rings his bicycle bell (*"Ring, ring!"*), and visits the vet. He says: *"Please heal this bird! I will pay with my work."*
  3. Act 3: In spring, the swallow brings a seed: *"Here is a golden seed for your kind heart."* Go Ne harvests gold coins.
  4. Act 4: Wen Shu grabs his slingshot (*"I want gold too!"*) and hits a bird. But when his seed grows, weeds and monsters appear!
  5. Act 5: Wen Shu weeps in remorse: *"I am so sorry!"* He leaves timber to fix Go Ne's roof. They shake hands as true friends.
- **Differentiation:**
  - *Support:* Pupils act as the village chorus repeating key past verbs: `He built! It fell! He paid! He gave!`
  - *Stretch:* Actors speak their dialogue using complete past-tense sentences.
- **Evidence of Learning:** Deep narrative retention of plot sequence, vocabulary, and moral values.

### Idea 5: The Frozen Forest Animal Freeze-Frame
- **Time:** 12 minutes
- **Prep:** Ambient winter wind sound on smartboard.
- **Materials:** None.
- **Target Words:** *freezing, -50°C, needles, snow, camouflage, hibernate, survive*.
- **Steps:**
  1. Pupils walk around the classroom mimicking different woodland creatures in summer.
  2. Teacher triggers a loud howling arctic blizzard sound on the smartboard: *"THE CONIFEROUS FREEZE HAS ARRIVED! MINUS FIFTY DEGREES!"*
  3. All pupils freeze in an adaptation posture:
     - Hares freeze flat against the floor (camouflaged).
     - Bears curl inside "dens" under desks (hibernating).
     - Pine trees stand tall with arms angled down like needles shedding snow.
  4. Teacher walks around tapping pupils to explain their posture: *"Why are your arms angled down?"* -> *"So the heavy snow slides off my branches without breaking them!"*
- **Differentiation:**
  - *Support:* Miming with single-word prompts (*Cold! Snow! Sleep!*).
  - *Stretch:* Full sentence explanations: `I hibernate in my den so I can survive the sub-zero winter.`
- **Evidence of Learning:** Applying scientific biome knowledge kinesthetically.

### Idea 6: The Vet Clinic Emergency Role-Play
- **Time:** 12 minutes
- **Prep:** 2 desks pushed together as an "Animal Examination Table".
- **Materials:** White toilet paper or cloth strips (bandages), toy doctor kit or wooden sticks (splints).
- **Target Words:** *vet, wing, injured, heal, care, paid, medicine, bicycle*.
- **Steps:**
  1. Pupils work in pairs: One is the compassionate rescuer; the other is the village vet.
  2. Rescuer: *"Doctor, look! A baby swallow fell from its high nest under my roof! Its wing is hurt."*
  3. Vet inspects: *"Let me check the wing. We will wrap it with a clean bandage and give it medicine."*
  4. Rescuer: *"How much does the medicine cost?"* Vet: *"Ten coins, please."*
  5. Rescuer mimes handing over coins: *"I paid the vet so the swallow can fly again!"*
- **Differentiation:**
  - *Support:* Provide dialog strips with blanks to fill in.
  - *Stretch:* Vet gives home care instructions: *"Keep the bird warm and feed it small worms."*
- **Evidence of Learning:** Oral practice of past irregular verb *paid* and medical context vocabulary.

## 🛠️ Category 3: Making & STEAM

### Idea 7: Shoebox Forest Biome Diorama (MANDATORY STEAM CRAFT)
- **Time:** 25 minutes
- **Prep:** Pupils bring an empty shoebox (or folded card stock) from home.
- **Materials:** Shoeboxes, green construction paper, twigs from the schoolyard, real pine needles and small pinecones, cotton balls (snow), scissors, glue.
- **Target Words:** *biome, deciduous, coniferous, needles, broad leaves, cones, snow, survive*.
- **Steps:**
  1. Pupils divide their shoebox into two halves with a cardboard divider:
     - Left Half: `DECIDUOUS FOREST` (Decorated with broad orange/red paper leaves, bare autumn twigs, and brown paper deer).
     - Right Half: `CONIFEROUS FOREST` (Decorated with real evergreen pine needles, small pinecones, cotton ball snow, and white paper snowshoe hares).
  2. Each pupil writes a mini-plaque for the front of the box:
     - `Deciduous trees drop broad leaves in autumn.`
     - `Coniferous trees have needles and cones instead of leaves to survive the -50°C cold.`
  3. Display the dioramas in the classroom window or school corridor as a STEAM gallery.
- **Differentiation:**
  - *Support:* Pre-cut animal silhouettes and leaf templates.
  - *Stretch:* Pupils write a 4-sentence scientific label comparing temperatures and adaptations.
- **Evidence of Learning:** Visual, tactile distinction between the two biomes and mastery of botanical features (*needles vs broad leaves*).

### Idea 8: The Winter Hibernation Story-Bag (MANDATORY ACTIVITY)
- **Time:** 15 minutes
- **Prep:** Brown paper lunch bags (or fabric drawstring pouches).
- **Materials:** Brown paper bags, cotton balls, dried leaves, miniature toy animals (or student paper cutouts of bears, bats, chipmunks, frogs).
- **Target Words:** *hibernate, den, energy, autumn, food, winter, wake up, survive*.
- **Steps:**
  1. Each pupil transforms a brown paper bag into a winter "den" by crumpling the edges, lining the inside with dried leaves and soft cotton wool.
  2. Pupils place their sleeping animal inside the den.
  3. Pupils write on the outside of the bag: `MY HIBERNATION DEN`.
  4. Pupil pairs present their story-bag to each other using the 3-step story frame:
     - *Step 1:* `In autumn, the bear eats berries to build thick fat.`
     - *Step 2:* `In winter, the bear sleeps in its den so it can save energy.`
     - *Step 3:* `In spring, the bear wakes up hungry when the snow melts.`
- **Differentiation:**
  - *Support:* Complete a 3-part cloze card glued to the paper bag.
  - *Stretch:* Describe what happens to the animal's heart rate and breathing during hibernation.
- **Evidence of Learning:** Clear narrative understanding of the biological process of hibernation.

### Idea 9: Golden Seed Origami Swallow
- **Time:** 15 minutes
- **Prep:** Square sheets of black or dark blue origami paper (15 cm x 15 cm), gold star stickers.
- **Materials:** Origami paper, yellow markers, gold star stickers (representing the magical seed).
- **Target Words:** *swallow, wings, tail, nest, golden seed, fly, return, friend*.
- **Steps:**
  1. Teacher demonstrates a simple 6-step origami swallow fold on the smartboard document camera.
  2. Pupils fold the wings and cut a small V in the tail to replicate the swallow's distinctive forked tail.
  3. Stick a gold star or draw a golden seed in the swallow's beak.
  4. Write on the swallow's wing: `Kindness brings true friendship.`
  5. Pupils hold their swallows aloft and make them "fly" to a classmate's desk, delivering a kind message: *"The swallow brought a golden seed for your kind heart!"*
- **Differentiation:**
  - *Support:* Teacher assists with the wing fold or provides pre-folded birds to decorate.
  - *Stretch:* Write a full sentence inside the swallow's wings describing Go Ne's good deeds.
- **Evidence of Learning:** Fine motor skills paired with thematic story appreciation.

## 🎵 Category 4: Movement, Songs & Original Chants

### Idea 10: Original Chant 1 — "The Two Biomes Beat" (WITH CLAPPING PATTERN)
- **Time:** 8 minutes
- **Prep:** Display lyrics on smartboard with seasonal tree icons.
- **Target Words:** *deciduous, coniferous, seasons, drop, needles, cones, instead of, so they can*.
- **Clapping Pattern:**
  - Slap chest with both hands (thump), clap hands (clap), snap fingers (snap)!
  - Pattern: *(thump-clap-snap!)*
- **Lyrics:**
  *(thump-clap-snap!)*  
  **Deciduous trees** have four seasons clear,  
  Broad green leaves through the warm of the year!  
  Autumn is coming, the orange leaves **drop**,  
  Saving their water when temperatures stop!  
  *(thump-clap-snap!)*  
  **Coniferous trees** in the freezing north grow,  
  Minus fifty degrees in the arctic wind blow!  
  They have sharp green **needles instead of flat leaves**,  
  And hard woody **cones** hanging high in the breeze!  
  *(Double Clap!)* **TREES ADAPT SO THEY CAN SURVIVE!**  
- **Differentiation:**
  - *Support:* Pupils join in on the chorus: *Instead of leaves! So they can survive!*
  - *Stretch:* Lead the chant with physical tree gestures (broad arms vs pointed fingers).
- **Evidence of Learning:** Pronunciation fluency of multi-syllable academic words (*de-cid-u-ous, con-if-er-ous*).

### Idea 11: Original Chant 2 — "The Vowel Team 'AI' Rain Train" (PHONICS CHANT & CLAPPING)
- **Time:** 8 minutes
- **Prep:** Smartboard displays steam train animations and word cards.
- **Target Words:** *rain, train, mail, tail, sail, paint, wait, main, chain*.
- **Clapping Pattern:**
  - Mimic a train chugging: Roll fists over each other twice *(chug-chug)*, pull whistle rope downward twice *(toot-toot!)*.
- **Lyrics:**
  Listen to the whistle on the railway track! *(chug-chug, toot-toot!)*  
  Vowel team **'ai'** has a long sound back!  
  **R-A-I-N** falls down on the plain!  
  Hop aboard the passenger **TRAIN!**  
  The postman delivers the morning **MAIL!**  
  The happy little dog wagged its fluffy **TAIL!**  
  White ships **SAIL** across the bay!  
  **PAINT** the fence without delay!  
  **WAIT** on the platform, don't miss the day!  
  *(Big Whistle!)* **CHOO-CHOO! LONG /eɪ/ ALL THE WAY!**  
- **Differentiation:**
  - *Support:* Check that Turkish pupils do NOT clip the sound to short /e/. Emphasize the glide /eɪ/.
  - *Stretch:* Pupils create their own rhyming couplet using *snail* and *trail*.
- **Evidence of Learning:** Accurate phonemic production of the vowel team `ai`.

### Idea 12: Original Chant 3 — "Go Ne and the Golden Swallow" (NARRATIVE STORY CHANT)
- **Time:** 8 minutes
- **Prep:** Display folk tale illustrations on smartboard.
- **Target Words:** *built, fell, rode, paid, gave, grew, hit, kind, greedy*.
- **Clapping & Stamping Pattern:**
  - 2 hand claps, followed by 1 desk tap: *(clap-clap-TAP!)*
- **Lyrics:**
  A swallow **built** a nest up high, *(clap-clap-TAP!)*  
  A baby **fell** down from the sky!  
  Kind Go Ne **rode** his bicycle fast,  
  He **paid** the vet till the storm had passed!  
  The bird came back and **gave** a seed,  
  It **grew** into gold for a gentle deed!  
  Wen Shu was greedy and **hit** a bird,  
  He learned his lesson from what occurred!  
  *(Double Clap!)* **KINDNESS WINS AND GREED MUST CEASE!**  
- **Differentiation:**
  - *Support:* Pupils shout out the past verbs on each desk tap.
  - *Stretch:* Pupils perform the chant with dramatic character gestures.
- **Evidence of Learning:** Internalization of irregular past-tense narrative verbs.

## 🗂️ Category 5: Stations & Centres

### Idea 13: Station 1 — The Biome Microscope Fact Lab
- **Time:** 12 minutes (rotation)
- **Prep:** Real pine needles, oak leaves, pinecones, magnifying glasses (or printed macro photographs).
- **Materials:** Hand lenses/magnifying glasses, real plant specimens, observation recording sheets.
- **Target Words:** *needle, broad leaf, cone, waxy, water, survive, adapt*.
- **Steps:**
  1. Pupils inspect a broad deciduous leaf and a sharp coniferous needle under hand lenses.
  2. Pupils touch the needle: Note the thick waxy coating and stiff shape.
  3. Pupils record on their sheet: `Needles are thin and waxy so they can save water in the freezing winter.`
  4. Compare the woody cone scales that protect the seeds from ice.
- **Evidence of Learning:** Direct scientific observation linked to functional language.

---

### Idea 14: Station 2 — The 'ai' vs Short /a/ Cargo Sorting Depot
- **Time:** 12 minutes (rotation)
- **Prep:** Two toy cargo train cars (or labeled cardboard boxes: `LONG /eɪ/ TRAIN` vs `SHORT /æ/ TRAM`).
- **Materials:** 20 word cargo crates (printed cards: *rain, ran, pain, pan, tail, tell, sail, sell, main, man, wait, wet, train, tram*).
- **Target Words:** *pain/pan, rain/ran, tail/tell, sail/sell, main/man*.
- **Steps:**
  1. Pupil draws a card, reads it aloud to their partner.
  2. Partner listens carefully: Is the vowel stretched long like *pain*, or clipped short like *pan*?
  3. Slot the cargo card into the correct train car.
  4. Both partners write the minimal pair in their notebooks.
- **Evidence of Learning:** Phonemic discrimination between English short /æ/ and diphthong /eɪ/.

---

### Idea 15: Station 3 — The Author's Purpose Publishing Desk
- **Time:** 12 minutes (rotation)
- **Prep:** A selection of real classroom books (science encyclopedia, folk tale storybook, travel brochure).
- **Materials:** Post-it notes, colored markers.
- **Target Words:** *author's purpose, inform, entertain, persuade, evidence, heading*.
- **Steps:**
  1. Pupils examine 3 different book covers and first pages.
  2. On a green Post-it note, pupils write:
     - Book Title: *Two Different Forests*
     - Author's Purpose: `TO INFORM`
     - Evidence: `It has headings, climate facts, and animal diagrams.`
  3. Stick the Post-it onto the book cover.
  4. Repeat for a storybook (`TO ENTERTAIN`).
- **Evidence of Learning:** Authentic analysis of informational vs narrative texts.
