# GRADE 3 UNIT 2 TEACHING IDEAS & ACTIVITIES
**English Adventure Academy — Global Readings 2: Unit 2 "Thank You"**  
*Audience:* Turkish Primary Learners (CEFR A1, Grade 3)  
*Setup:* Exactly ONE Interactive Smartboard + Low-Cost Classroom Materials  

---

## 🗣️ Category 1: Speaking Games

### Idea 1: The Gratitude Hot Potato (Pass the Harvest)
- **Time:** 10 minutes
- **Prep:** None (use a soft tennis ball or crumpled paper ball).
- **Materials:** Soft ball or toy beanbag, timer on smartboard.
- **Target Words:** *thankful for, family, food, home, friends, teacher, books, games*.
- **Steps:**
  1. Teacher sets a 60-second smartboard countdown timer with cheerful festival music.
  2. Pupils sit in their rows or a circle. Pupils pass the ball to the pupil next to them.
  3. When the teacher taps "PAUSE" on the smartboard, the pupil holding the ball stands up.
  4. Pupil completes the target frame: `I am thankful for [noun] because [reason].` (e.g., *"I am thankful for my cat because it is soft."*)
  5. The class responds in chorus: *"Thank you, [Name]!"* Resume passing.
- **Differentiation:**
  - *Support:* Board displays 6 picture icons with labels. Pupil points and reads: `I am thankful for my dog.`
  - *Stretch:* Pupil must name two items and use *because*: `I am thankful for my brother and my sister because they play with me.`
- **Evidence of Learning:** Teacher checks oral accuracy of the preposition *for* (not *to* or omission) and correct noun vocabulary.

### Idea 2: Space Suit Airlock Intercom
- **Time:** 12 minutes
- **Prep:** Print 4 paper badges labeled "Mission Control" and "Mars Farmer".
- **Materials:** Paper cups or plastic cups (toy microphones), smartboard airlock graphic.
- **Target Words:** *space suit, helmet, air, greenhouse, limited, crops, potatoes, corn*.
- **Steps:**
  1. Smartboard displays a flashing red/green airlock status screen (`OXYGEN: CHECKED | PRESSURE: NORMAL`).
  2. Pupil A acts as Mission Control at the front desk; Pupil B acts as the Mars Scout by the classroom door.
  3. Pupil A speaks into the paper cup: *"Scout August! Check your space suit! Do you have air?"*
  4. Pupil B inspects their uniform and replies: *"Yes, Mission Control! My helmet is locked. There are pink potatoes ready for harvest!"*
  5. Rotate roles every 2 minutes across table pairs.
- **Differentiation:**
  - *Support:* Provide a written two-line script strip on the desk.
  - *Stretch:* Pupil must add a warning about limited resources: *"Hurry! The water is limited. Close the dome door!"*
- **Evidence of Learning:** Pupil accurately pronounces *space suit*, *helmet*, and uses *There are* with plural crops.

### Idea 3: The King's Weaving Contest Debate
- **Time:** 15 minutes
- **Prep:** Divide class into "Team Red" and "Team Blue".
- **Materials:** Red and blue paper ribbons or colored chalk on board.
- **Target Words:** *weave, cloth, contest, king, feast, proud, colorful*.
- **Steps:**
  1. Smartboard displays the King of Silla seated on a golden throne under the full moon.
  2. Team Red shows their imaginary red cloth: *"Look, King! Our cloth is red and soft! We weaved for one month!"*
  3. Team Blue shows their imaginary blue cloth: *"Look, King! Our cloth is blue and bright! We worked hard together!"*
  4. Teacher picks a pupil to wear a paper crown and act as King. The King inspects both sides and declares: *"Both cloths are beautiful! Let us have a grand feast under the full moon!"*
- **Differentiation:**
  - *Support:* Pupils hold up colored paper strips and say: `We weave red cloth!`
  - *Stretch:* Pupils use comparative vocabulary: `Our cloth is softer and warmer!`
- **Evidence of Learning:** Oral production of action verb *weave* and narrative noun *contest*.

## 🎭 Category 2: Drama & Role-Play

### Idea 4: The Chuseok Family Reunion Mime
- **Time:** 12 minutes
- **Prep:** 4 role cards (Grandparent, Parent, Child, Ancestor).
- **Materials:** None (body language and classroom props: desks as dinner tables).
- **Target Words:** *ancestors, ceremony, songpyeon, feast, travel, bow, respect*.
- **Steps:**
  1. Teacher explains the traditional Korean bow of deep respect (*sebae/charye*) performed during ancestor ceremonies.
  2. Groups of 4 pupils assign roles: Grandparent sits at desk; Parents arrange paper plates; Children enter from the door ("traveling home for the holiday").
  3. Children bow respectfully: *"Happy Chuseok! We are thankful for our ancestors."*
  4. Grandparent offers imaginary songpyeon: *"Welcome home! Eat delicious rice cakes."*
  5. The family sits together and mimes enjoying a harvest feast.
- **Differentiation:**
  - *Support:* Miming with 1 short choral line: `Thank you, family!`
  - *Stretch:* Children describe 3 foods on the table: `There is fruit, there is rice, and there are sweet cakes.`
- **Evidence of Learning:** Understanding cultural significance of ancestor remembrance and polite family language.

### Idea 5: Freeze-Frame: The Mars Harvest Surprise!
- **Time:** 15 minutes
- **Prep:** Set up 3 story scenes on the smartboard (Scene 1: Dad looking worried at dry soil; Scene 2: Digging up pink potatoes; Scene 3: Putting on space helmets).
- **Materials:** Whiteboard markers, classroom props (backpacks as oxygen tanks).
- **Target Words:** *worried, limited, believe, stripes, helmet, outside, setting*.
- **Steps:**
  1. Pupils form table groups of 4. Teacher announces: *"Scene 1: Dad is worried! 3-2-1 FREEZE!"*
  2. Groups freeze in dramatic poses showing worry, holding empty water cups, inspecting dry plants.
  3. Teacher taps a frozen pupil on the shoulder. The pupil unfreezes and says: *"I am worried! The water is limited!"*
  4. Teacher calls: *"Scene 2: Digging pink potatoes! 3-2-1 FREEZE!"* Tapped pupil unfreezes: *"I cannot believe my eyes! The corn is purple!"*
  5. Teacher calls: *"Scene 3: The space helmet reveal! FREEZE!"* Tapped pupil: *"Check your helmet! We are on Mars!"*
- **Differentiation:**
  - *Support:* Pupils hold emotion emoji cards (😟 for worried, 😲 for amazed).
  - *Stretch:* Unfrozen pupils explain the reason: *"I am amazed because vegetables on Earth are not purple!"*
- **Evidence of Learning:** Expressive intonation matching story moods and correct recall of narrative sequence.

### Idea 6: The Weaving Loom Human Shuttle
- **Time:** 10 minutes
- **Prep:** Clear a narrow center aisle in the classroom.
- **Materials:** Two long lengths of cheap yarn or skipping ropes.
- **Target Words:** *weave, under, over, thread, cloth, team, fast*.
- **Steps:**
  1. Six pupils stand in a straight line representing the "warp threads" of a giant loom.
  2. Two "shuttle runners" hold a ball of colorful yarn.
  3. The class chants: *"Over, under, weave the thread! Make the cloth of royal red!"*
  4. The shuttle runners weave in and out of the standing pupils, alternating going in front and behind.
  5. Once through, they hold up the woven pattern: *"Look at our cloth! We are fast weavers!"*
- **Differentiation:**
  - *Support:* Walking slowly while repeating: `Over, under, over, under.`
  - *Stretch:* Describe the cloth in complete sentences: `We weaved soft cloth for the king's contest.`
- **Evidence of Learning:** Physical comprehension of the action *weave* (crossing over and under).

## 🛠️ Category 3: Making & STEAM

### Idea 7: Mars Dome Greenhouse in a Plastic Cup (MANDATORY STEAM CRAFT)
- **Time:** 20 minutes
- **Prep:** Teacher collects clear plastic drink cups and small cotton balls/soil.
- **Materials:** Clear plastic cups (2 per pair: one as base, one as inverted dome), cotton balls or potting soil, fast-sprouting beans or grass seeds, water dropper, permanent markers.
- **Target Words:** *greenhouse, dome, plant, water, limited, grow, crop, Mars*.
- **Steps:**
  1. Teacher displays the Mars greenhouse dome graphic on the smartboard.
  2. Each pupil labels their clear cup: `MARS DOME BASE 3000 — [PUPIL NAME]`.
  3. Pupils place cotton wool at the bottom, add 2 beans, and carefully add exactly 3 drops of water with a dropper.
  4. Teacher reinforces: *"Water on Mars is limited! Only three drops, not ten!"*
  5. Invert a second clear cup on top and seal with tape to form a sealed greenhouse dome.
  6. Place on the sunny windowsill. Over the next 5 days, water condensates inside the dome just like a real biosphere!
- **Differentiation:**
  - *Support:* Pupil copies the label: `There is a bean in my dome.`
  - *Stretch:* Pupil keeps a daily 1-sentence log: `Day 1: Water is limited. Day 3: A small green leaf is growing.`
- **Evidence of Learning:** Scientific understanding of greenhouse conservation and usage of *limited* and *grow*.

### Idea 8: Play-Dough Songpyeon Craft Workshop (MANDATORY CRAFT — NO REAL FOOD)
- **Time:** 15 minutes
- **Prep:** Make or buy cheap non-toxic play-dough in pastel green, pink, and white.
- **Materials:** Play-dough balls, plastic rolling pins or rulers, paper plates, toothpicks.
- **Target Words:** *songpyeon, rice cake, moon, half-moon, feast, shape, dough*.
- **Steps:**
  1. Smartboard displays traditional half-moon shaped songpyeon rice cakes on pine needles.
  2. Teacher demonstrates rolling a small ball of dough, flattening it into a circle, folding it in half, and pinching the edges into a crescent (half-moon shape).
  3. Pupils chant: *"Roll it round like the full moon, pinch it curved like the half moon!"*
  4. Each pupil makes 3 play-dough songpyeon cakes (1 pink, 1 green, 1 white) and arranges them on a paper plate.
  5. Pupils present their plate to a partner: `There are three songpyeon on my plate. They are in the shape of a half moon.`
- **Differentiation:**
  - *Support:* Trace a half-moon template on paper before shaping the dough.
  - *Stretch:* Pupil explains why they are shaped like half-moons: `We eat songpyeon because they look like the moon.`
- **Evidence of Learning:** Tactile vocabulary acquisition (*half-moon, shape, rice cake*) and correct plural agreement (*There are three...*).

### Idea 9: Space Helmet Visor Blueprint
- **Time:** 15 minutes
- **Prep:** Print circular helmet visor templates on A4 paper.
- **Materials:** Paper plates or A4 printed templates, crayons, aluminum foil strips.
- **Target Words:** *helmet, space suit, air, glass, look, outside, protect*.
- **Steps:**
  1. Cut out the center oval of a paper plate to create an astronaut face visor.
  2. Pupils decorate the outer rim with buttons: `AIR: 100%`, `COMM: ON`, `RADAR: OK`.
  3. Pupils hold the visor in front of their faces, look at the smartboard Martian landscape, and report:
     - *"Look through the visor! There is red sand outside!"*
     - *"My helmet protects my head and gives me air!"*
- **Differentiation:**
  - *Support:* Pupil labels two parts: `helmet` and `air`.
  - *Stretch:* Pupil writes a 3-step safety check on the back: `1. Check air. 2. Lock helmet. 3. Open door.`
- **Evidence of Learning:** Functional understanding of story technology terms (*helmet, air*).

## 🎵 Category 4: Movement, Songs & Original Chants

### Idea 10: Original Chant 1 — "The Full Moon Weaving Beat" (WITH CLAPPING PATTERN)
- **Time:** 8 minutes
- **Prep:** Display lyrics on smartboard with rhythmic icons.
- **Target Words:** *harvest, weave, cloth, contest, full moon, king, feast*.
- **Clapping Pattern:**
  - Beat 1: Slap knees (pat)
  - Beat 2: Clap hands together (clap)
  - Beat 3: Snap fingers or tap desk (snap)
  - Beat 4: Rest / Open palms
- **Lyrics:**
  *(Pat, Clap, Snap, Rest!)*  
  Autumn is here, the **harvest** is done!  
  *(Pat, Clap, Snap, Rest!)*  
  We gather the crops in the bright autumn sun!  
  *(Pat, Clap, Snap, Rest!)*  
  **Weave, weave, weave** the colorful **cloth**!  
  Two teams working, nothing is lost!  
  *(Pat, Clap, Snap, Rest!)*  
  Look up high at the **full, round moon**!  
  The **king** gives a **feast** with a golden spoon!  
  *(Double Clap!)* **HAPPY CHUSEOK!**  
- **Differentiation:**
  - *Support:* Focus on clapping on the bold vocabulary words.
  - *Stretch:* Pupil leads the rhythm at the front of the room.
- **Evidence of Learning:** Rhythm awareness, correct word stress on *harvest* and *contest*.

### Idea 11: Original Chant 2 — "The Sharp -ST Sound Vault" (PHONICS CHANT & CLAPPING)
- **Time:** 8 minutes
- **Prep:** Display -st phonics cards on smartboard.
- **Target Words:** *fast, last, lost, best, test, harvest, first*.
- **Clapping Pattern:**
  - 2 quick desk pats, followed by ONE sharp, loud hand clap right on the /st/ sound!
  - Pattern: *(pat-pat-CLAP!)*
- **Lyrics:**
  Run, run, run, the rabbit is **FAST!** *(pat-pat-CLAP!)*  
  Don't be slow, don't be the **LAST!** *(pat-pat-CLAP!)*  
  Where is my key? Oh no, it is **LOST!** *(pat-pat-CLAP!)*  
  Study real hard, be the very **BEST!** *(pat-pat-CLAP!)*  
  Pencil in hand, take the English **TEST!** *(pat-pat-CLAP!)*  
  Gather the corn, it's the autumn **HARVEST!** *(pat-pat-CLAP!)*  
  Number one runner, who crossed **FIRST?** *(pat-pat-CLAP!)*  
  Stop that /t/ sharp, don't let it burst!  
- **Differentiation:**
  - *Support:* Check that Turkish pupils do NOT say *fast-ı* on the clap. The clap stops the vocal cord immediately!
  - *Stretch:* Pupils create their own rhyming line (e.g. *A bird in the nest!*).
- **Evidence of Learning:** Elimination of Turkish epenthetic final vowel on consonant clusters.

### Idea 12: Original Chant 3 — "The Mars Dome Harvest Hop" (MOVEMENT CHANT)
- **Time:** 8 minutes
- **Prep:** None.
- **Target Words:** *greenhouse, potatoes, corn, space suit, helmet, Mars*.
- **Clapping & Stamping Pattern:**
  - Stamp right foot, stamp left foot, jump with two feet!
- **Lyrics:**
  Step inside the **dome**, what do you see?  
  Pink, pink **potatoes** as sweet as can be! *(Stamp-Stamp-Jump!)*  
  Look at the stalk, the **corn** is purple bright!  
  Water is **limited**, but growing just right! *(Stamp-Stamp-Jump!)*  
  Check your **space suit**, lock on your **helmet** tight!  
  We're farming on **Mars** under cosmic night! *(Double Jump!)*  
- **Differentiation:**
  - *Support:* Pupils jump in place and repeat the color words: *pink, purple*.
  - *Stretch:* Pupils recite the chant while miming wearing heavy space boots.
- **Evidence of Learning:** Memory recall of the story's visual oddities (pink potatoes, purple corn).

## 🗂️ Category 5: Stations & Centres

### Idea 13: Station 1 — The Setting Sorting Station
- **Time:** 12 minutes (rotation)
- **Prep:** Two labeled plastic trays: `EARTH (KOREA)` and `SPACE (MARS)`.
- **Materials:** 12 laminated picture cards (full moon, hanbok, loom, songpyeon, rover, space suit, helmet, pink potato, purple corn, oxygen tank, apple pie, village).
- **Target Words:** *setting, Earth, Mars, space suit, loom, feast, dome*.
- **Steps:**
  1. Small groups visit Station 1. Pupils pick a card, say the word aloud, and place it in the correct tray.
  2. Pupil must justify the placement: `The space suit belongs to Mars because there is no air.`
  3. Both partners record their score on their station passport.
- **Evidence of Learning:** Reading skill mastery (*Understanding the Setting*).

---

### Idea 14: Station 2 — The -st Phonics Minimal Pair Balance Scale
- **Time:** 12 minutes (rotation)
- **Prep:** A simple toy balance scale (or two paper cups hung on a coat hanger).
- **Materials:** Word cards with `-s` / `-t` vs `-st` (pass vs past, bet vs best, ten vs test, fat vs fast, lot vs lost).
- **Target Words:** *fast, last, lost, best, test, past*.
- **Steps:**
  1. Pupil reads Card A (*bet*) and Card B (*best*).
  2. Pupil identifies which card has the sharp consonant blend `-st`.
  3. Pupil drops a plastic counter into the `-st` cup only when pronounced with a crisp, clipped ending.
  4. Aim: Collect 10 counters in the -st cup!
- **Evidence of Learning:** Phonemic awareness and pronunciation precision.

---

### Idea 15: Station 3 — The Gratitude Quilt Collaborative Poster
- **Time:** 12 minutes (rotation)
- **Prep:** A large square sheet of butcher paper divided into 20 square "tiles" like a woven cloth.
- **Materials:** Crayons, colored paper squares, glue stick.
- **Target Words:** *thankful for, weave, cloth, share, family, food, school*.
- **Steps:**
  1. Each pupil receives one paper tile (10 cm x 10 cm).
  2. Pupil draws one thing they are grateful for and writes the sentence at the bottom: `I am thankful for [my mom].`
  3. Pupils glue their tile onto the giant class poster to create the "Class 3A Harvest Quilt".
  4. Hang the finished collaborative quilt under the smartboard.
- **Evidence of Learning:** Written accuracy of the target grammar formula in a proud, visible format.
