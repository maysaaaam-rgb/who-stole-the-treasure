These are classroom bands made for this quiz. They match the A1 to A1+ descriptors in a simple way. They are not an official CEFR certificate. The quiz tests English in new situations, not memory of the stories. Use the unit bands to see where each pupil needs support; the speaking lesson next time adds speaking.

After the quiz: what to practise
If a pupil is below A1+ in...Practise
Unit 1Past simple, regular and irregular (chased, fell, rode, had, caught); Who / Where / Why questions; reading words (skim, journey).
Unit 2“so I can / so it can”, irregular past (built, gave, grew, paid), nature words (deciduous, coniferous, hibernate).
Unit 3Present continuous (is melting, am writing), was / were, adjectives with and / but (noisy, quiet, expensive, dirty).
Unit 4Past simple in sport and school situations (won, scored, performed), because and so, words (court, league, calm).
