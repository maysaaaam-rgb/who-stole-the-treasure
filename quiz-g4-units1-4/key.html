These are classroom bands made for this quiz. They match the A1 to A1+ descriptors in a simple way. They are not an official CEFR certificate. Use the unit bands to see where each pupil needs support; the speaking lesson next time adds the fourth skill.

After the quiz: what to practise
If a pupil is below A1+ in...Practise
Unit 1Past verbs (chased, fell), Who / Where / Why questions, skim and workout.
Unit 2“so it can / so they can”, deciduous and coniferous, built, gave, grew, hit, paid.
Unit 3The ice is melting, was thin, adjectives with and / but, car words (gasoline, electricity).
Unit 4Past simple (scored, won, performed), because, league, basket, platform.
