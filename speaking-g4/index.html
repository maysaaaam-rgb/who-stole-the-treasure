The lesson (35 minutes, one by one)

  • 0–5 min · Rules and model. Tell the class: “You will talk to me for one minute. Four short questions. It is OK to make mistakes.” Model one task with a pupil: show a good answer and a short answer.
  • 5–10 min · Pair practice. Hand out the printed practice cards (they are NOT the test questions). In pairs: A asks, B answers in a full sentence, then swap.
  • 10–32 min · The check. Pairs keep practising and change partners every 4 minutes. You sit at the laptop, call one pupil, press Start. Each pupil needs about 1 minute: 4 tasks, 10 to 15 seconds each. Do not correct during the check.
  • 32–35 min · Close. Everybody stands and says one sentence they are proud of. Tell them the result is private and it helps you choose the next practice.

How the marks work (each task 0 to 2)

  • 2 = a clear full sentence and the target form is correct (small slips are fine).
  • 1 = you understand, but the form is wrong or missing, or the answer is one word or needed the hint.
  • 0 = no answer or not understandable.
  • If you press 💡 Show hint, the mark is 1 at most (unless Support mode is on).
  • Speaking levels: 0–2 Below A1 · 3–4 A1 · 5–6 A1+ · 7–8 A1+ strong. These are classroom bands, not an official CEFR result.

Tips

  • Each pupil gets different question versions, so the pupil before cannot pass the answers.
  • Start with a confident pupil, then mix strong and quiet pupils. Smile and wait 5 seconds before showing the hint.
  • Use Support mode for any pupil who needs extra time or help; give extra time and quiet space. Keep notes about English only.
  • Nothing leaves this computer. Press Copy in Results to paste the marks into your own sheet.
