How it works (about 3 minutes per pair)

Task 1 · Question and answer (pair)Pupil A draws a card and asks. Pupil B answers with a full past sentence. Then swap. Listen for: did + base verb in the question, past verb in the answer.
Task 2 · Tell it (alone, 30 seconds)Choose one story. Say 3 or 4 sentences with First, Then, After that, Finally. Use the verb bank for help.
Task 3 · Role-play (pair)
Scoring (1 to 3 for each)1 = needs help · 2 = good · 3 = excellent. Praise self-corrections (“He stoled... no, stole!”). A wrong verb never loses points; it only shows what to practise next.
