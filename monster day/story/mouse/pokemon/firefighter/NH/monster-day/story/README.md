# Step into the Story: The Wizard of Oz
### Interactive Classroom Stage for Primary English (CEFR A1+)
**Lesson Duration:** 30–40 minutes  
**Target Level:** Primary Young Learners (CEFR A1+)  
**Teacher's Role:** Storyteller, Narrator & Stage Director  
**Students' Role:** Listeners ➔ Predictors ➔ Characters ➔ Speakers ➔ Retellers  

---

## 🚀 How to Run in the Classroom

1. Simply open `index.html` in any modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox) on your Interactive Whiteboard, Projector, Laptop, or Tablet.
2. No installation, server, or internet connection is required — all audio synthesis and vector illustrations work 100% offline.

## ⌨️ Teacher Keyboard Shortcuts

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `Space` or `→` | **Next** scene / Next step |
| `←` | **Back** to previous scene / step |
| `T` | Toggle **Teacher Guide & Narration Drawer** |
| `M` | Toggle **Sound Effects On / Off** |
| `F` | Toggle **Fullscreen Stage Mode** |

## 📚 12 Integrated Lesson Modules

1. **✨ Part 1: Enter the Story** — Visual clues & 5 key vocabulary cards (STORM, HOUSE, FOREST, SCARED, ROAD) with physical TPR actions.
2. **🔮 Part 2: Prediction Introduction** — Mysterious first scene, 3 visual choices, and pair prediction sentence frame (*"I think Dorothy will..."*).
3. **📖 Part 3: Story Mode (11 Scenes + 3 Story Stops)** — Complete animated storybook stage with interactive props (such as the clickable Oil Can for Tin Man).
4. **🎭 Part 4: Character Mode** — Character cards (Dorothy, Toto, Scarecrow, Tin Man, Lion, Wizard, Witch) with feelings, goals, lines, and *"YOUR TURN"* floating prompts.
5. **💬 Part 5: Pair Talk** — Student-to-student speaking dialogues with structured sentence frames.
6. **🗺️ Part 6: Story Decisions** — Major decision points with problem presentation, pair talk, voting, and story outcome reveals.
7. **🧩 Part 7: Post-Story 1 — Story Memory** — 8 visual event cards to sequence from 1 to 8 with instant feedback and sentence frames (*First... Then... Next... Finally...*).
8. **🎨 Part 8: Post-Story 2 — Picture Retell** — Image-only stage with a 3-tier teacher hint system (*Keywords ➔ Sentence Starter ➔ Model Retell*).
9. **🪑 Part 9: Post-Story 3 — Character Hot Seat** — Interactive question menu for the class and character response support.
10. **💡 Part 10: Post-Story 4 — What If...?** — Creative alternative story generator with A1+ sentence structures.
11. **🎬 Part 11: Classroom Mini-Theatre** — 4 group scenes with rehearsal countdown timer (60s) and grand celebration finale (*"GREAT STORYTELLERS!"*).
12. **📋 Part 12: Teacher Observation & Assessment** — Quick-tap evaluation grid tracking *Listening, Speaking, Interaction, Prediction, Role-Play, and Retelling*.
