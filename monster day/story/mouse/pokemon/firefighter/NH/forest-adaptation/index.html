🌲 Biome Cold-Adaptation: Broadleaves vs. Evergreen Needles

🍁 Deciduous Forests

Broad, flat leaves change to vibrant red and gold in autumn, then fall down to conserve moisture through winter.

🍂

🌲 Coniferous Forests

Thin, needle-shaped waxy leaves stay deep green through freezing blizzards and don't fall off in autumn.

The Author's Purpose Detective Wheel

Every book, article, and poem is written for a distinct goal:

🎭

TO ENTERTAIN

Makes you laugh, have fun, feel thrilled, or imagine magical worlds!

📖

TO TEACH / INFORM

Gives real science facts, nature secrets, historical dates, or explanations.

📢

TO PERSUADE

Tries to change your opinion, convince your mind, or urge you to act!
