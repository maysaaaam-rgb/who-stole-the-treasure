TEACHER'S LESSON GUIDE (A1+ GRADE 4)

Classroom Flow (35-40 Minutes):

  1. 0:00 - 0:05: 🚨 Pokémon Emergency & Spotting icebreaker (warm-up, high energy).
  2. 0:05 - 0:15: 📖 Adjectives & Abilities vocabulary teaching with TPR physical gestures (stretch, flex, fly).
  3. 0:15 - 0:20: 🗣️ Sentence modeling ("It is small and fast. It can jump and make electricity.").
  4. 0:20 - 0:32: 🧬 Character Creator & Speaking Gate. Call pairs of students to smartboard to create, describe aloud, and battle!
  5. 0:32 - 0:38: ⚔️ English Battle: Spoken English earns attack power! Whole class ticks their Pokédex.
  6. 0:38 - 0:40: 🏆 Champion Badge ceremony & independent drawing sheet.
