💡 THE TIME RULE:
Today: Alice goes to school. ➔ Yesterday: Alice WENT to Wonderland!

1. Alice W E N T to Wonderland.
2. Alice S A W the White Rabbit.
3. Alice O P E N E D the secret door.
4. Alice F O U N D a golden key.
5. Alice M E T the Mad Hatter.
