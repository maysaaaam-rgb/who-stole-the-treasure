🧑‍🏫 TEACHER GUIDE & SPEAKING RUBRIC

Lesson Plan: 45–50 Minutes • Level: A1+ Young Learners

Classroom Flow (10 Stages)
1. Mystery Face (5m)Hook with morphing emoji faces (😡, 😨).
2. Feeling Discovery (5m)6 Core + 6 A1+ emotions with gestures.
3. Which Feeling? (5m)5-Second THINK timer before choosing.
4. Main Activity (10m)10 situational cartoon dilemmas.
5. Harder Thinking (5m)Dilemmas with multiple valid answers.
6. Spin & React (7m)Wheel spins for situation ➔ Feel ➔ Act.
7. Match Triplet (5m)Connect Situation ➔ Feeling ➔ Action.
8. Feelings Song (5m)3 Rounds: Listen, Gesture, Predict.
9. Boss Challenge (5m)Rapid-fire 5-second reaction drill.
10. Exit Ticket (3m)Individual spoken check & awards.
Differentiated Speaking Rubric
🟢 Tier 1 (Emerging):
Understands question. Responds with emotion word or short phrase:
"Happy" or "I'm scared."
🟡 Tier 2 (Standard A1+):
Produces hypothetical chunk with correct emotion:
"I'd feel scared."
🟣 Tier 3 (Extension):
Produces full emotion + action response:
"I'd feel scared. I'd run away."
