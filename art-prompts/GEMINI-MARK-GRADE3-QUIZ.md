## Step 2: the answers (use the right form!)

**Game 1: Ice-Bridge Stones, 15 points, 3 for each step. The pupil circles one of two stones. Count the circle that is on the stone with the correct words:**

- Step 2: "Twice the man's size" (not "Half the man's size")

- Step 5: "the sun" (not "gasoline")
(The position of the right stone is different in Form A and Form B, so read the words, not the position.)

**Game 2 Part A: the maze, 8 points, all or nothing.** The pupil circles one igloo under the maze ("Safe exit"). Correct: **Form A = Igloo #4. Form B = Igloo #2.** (8 points for the right igloo, 0 for any other.)

**Game 2 Part B: word match, 12 points, 2 each.** The pupil writes a letter in the box next to each word:
- Form A: 1 enemy = C, 2 trap = E, 3 melting = F, 4 gasoline = A, 5 energy = D, 6 expensive = B
- Form B: 1 enemy = B, 2 trap = D, 3 melting = A, 4 gasoline = F, 5 energy = C, 6 expensive = E

**Game 3: the snake, 10 points.** The pupil draws one line from START to FINISH, up, down, left or right (no diagonals), through the 8 words from Unit 3, and does not touch the 4 words that are not from the unit.
- The 8 right words are: BEAR, ICE, THIN, HOLE, FISH, TRAP, ENEMY, MELTING. The 4 wrong words are: PENCIL, BANANA, BOOK, APPLE.
- Form A: the line goes through boxes #1 → #2 → #6 → #5 → #9 → #10 → #11 → #12.
- Form B: the line goes through boxes #4 → #3 → #7 → #8 → #12 → #11 → #10 → #9.
- Marking: **1 point for each of the 8 right words the line passes through** (maximum 8). **A word is not counted if the line touches one of the 4 wrong words** at that place. **Plus 2 points for the 4 picture boxes**: the pupil must write the word under the picture (BEAR, HOLE, TRAP, MELTING): 0.5 point for each correct word. Small spelling mistakes are fine if the word is clearly the right one (for example "bere" is not fine, "enemmy" is fine); write the spelling mistakes in the notes.
- If the line is hard to follow, mark what you can follow and write "snake unclear" in "teacher must check".

**Game 3: the -ng sound, 5 points.** The pupil circles words that end with the -ng sound. Correct: **fishing, melting, living** (not winter, twice, danger). Marking: 1 point for each of the 3 right words circled (maximum 3), plus 2 points if **no wrong word** is circled.

**Part 4: Listening, 12 points, 3 each.** The pupil ticks one box for each of the 4 questions. (In Form B the 4 rows are in a different order: read the words.)

**Part 5 B: car table, 10 points, 2 for each blank. The pupil writes the missing word.**
