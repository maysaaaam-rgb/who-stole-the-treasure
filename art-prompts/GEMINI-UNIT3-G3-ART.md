## Picture types and sizes (PNG, use the exact file names)
**Type A: objects and characters.** Square 1:1, 1024×1024, ONE subject (or one small group where the name says so), centred, filling about 80% of the frame, on ONE flat plain light-grey background (#e8e8e8), no floor, no ground shadow, thick white sticker outline around the whole subject.
**Type B: backdrops.** Wide 2:1, 1600×800, a full scene with NO people, NO animals and NO text, same soft 3D-rendered style, no outline, a calm lower third (ground) so that characters can be placed on top.
**Type C: story scenes.** 4:3, 1600×1200, a full scene WITH the characters named, no text, no outline, same soft 3D style, clear and simple composition (one main action) so that a child understands the moment at a glance.
