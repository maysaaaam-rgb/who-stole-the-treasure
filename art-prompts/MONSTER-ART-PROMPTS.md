# Monster art prompt kit (Gemini app, Ultra plan)

Goal: 5 species x 5 stages = 25 monsters, plus 5 eggs. Every image must match in style.

## How to use (important for matching style)
1. Open ONE new chat in the Gemini app. Use the same chat for the whole species so the look stays consistent.
2. Paste the STYLE BLOCK first, then say: "Remember this style for all my next images."
3. For each image paste the species line + stage line. If you like a result, say "use this as the reference for the next stage" and give the next stage line.
4. Ask for 1:1 square (1024x1024) every time. Download as PNG.
5. Save as `species_stage.png` (example: `emberwing_baby.png`) in `assets/monsters/new/`. Tell me when done; I connect them.
6. Transparent background: Gemini usually gives a plain background. Use remove.bg or Photoshop/Canva "remove background" afterwards, or say "I will cut out the background, so keep it a single flat colour with no shadow and no gradient".

## STYLE BLOCK (paste once per chat)
```
You are the art director of a children's English learning game for ages 8 to 10.
Draw original cute creature mascots in ONE consistent style:
- polished 3D-rendered mobile-game mascot look (like a modern collectible-monster game), soft studio lighting from the top left, subtle rim light, glossy big eyes with two white highlights
- chibi proportions: large head, round body, tiny arms and feet, friendly happy expression, no teeth showing, never scary
- thick soft clean outline, rich saturated but child-friendly colours, smooth shading, no photo realism, no text, no watermark, no logo
- full body, front three-quarter view, centered, facing the viewer, whole creature visible with space around it
- one flat plain light-grey background (#e8e8e8), no ground shadow, no floor, no props unless I say so
- square 1:1 image
Every creature belongs to a species and grows through 5 stages: baby, growing, adventurer, advanced, ultimate. Keep the same face, colours and signature features across stages so a child recognises the same pet growing up. Each stage is a little bigger, more confident and more decorated than the last.
```

## STAGE LINES (add after the species line)
- **baby:** "Stage 1 BABY: very small and round, oversized head, stubby limbs, the signature feature only just starting (tiny nubs/buds), big curious eyes, sleepy-cute."
- **growing:** "Stage 2 GROWING: a bit taller, signature feature small but clear, playful pose, one tiny accessory (a little scarf or bandage on the arm)."
- **adventurer:** "Stage 3 ADVENTURER: explorer look, slightly slimmer body, signature feature fully visible, wearing a small adventurer satchel, confident smile, brave pose."
- **advanced:** "Stage 4 ADVANCED: strong and graceful, larger signature feature with a glowing detail, a small shining badge or armor piece, heroic pose, still friendly."
- **ultimate:** "Stage 5 ULTIMATE: the grand final form, majestic but still cute and child-friendly, big magical signature feature, crown-like or aura detail, glowing elemental particles around it, proud pose."
