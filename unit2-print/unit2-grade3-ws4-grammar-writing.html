📐 Language Formulas:
• There is a + ONE thing (singular)  |  There are + TWO OR MORE things (plural)
• I am thankful for + [noun]  |  Why? -> Because + [reason]

Part 2: Why? Because... Match ❓💡 4 marks

Match the Why question to the correct Because reason. Write A, B, C, or D in the box.

1. Why was Dad worried?
A. Because there is no air outside.
2. Why do they wear space suits?
B. Because there was very little water.
3. Why do families celebrate Chuseok?
C. Because she wanted to make cloth.
4. Why did the girl weave thread?
D. Because they give thanks for the harvest.
