Teacher Guide: English Progress Check · Grade 3 · Unit 2

Pupil paper: unit2-grade3-check50.html · Content: Global Readings 2 Unit 2: "Thank You" (pp. 24–39).

Written Paper: 38 marks, about 38 minutes (A: 8 · B: 8 · C: 6 · D: 5 · E: 6 · F: 5). Speaking: 12 marks, 2 minutes per pupil in small groups. Total: 50 marks.

1. Listening Script (Read each item clearly TWICE at natural speed)

Say: "Section A. Listening. Put your finger on A1. Listen and circle the picture. Number 1."

A1.1: "Look up at the night sky. The full moon is big and bright." (pause 5 seconds, read again)

A1.2: "We picked purple corn in the greenhouse." (pause 5 seconds, read again)

A1.3: "Two teams weave colorful cloth with thread." (pause 5 seconds, read again)

A1.4: "Put on your space suit and helmet before you go outside." (pause 5 seconds, read again)

Say: "Now look at A2. Listen to the story about the First Harvest. Number the pictures 1, 2, 3, 4."

A2 Story: "The family lives on a new farm. First, Dad was worried because there was very little water. Second, the family dug up big pink potatoes and green crops. Third, Mom baked a delicious apple pie for their harvest feast. Last, before Ruth and August played outside, they put on their space suits and helmets. Hooray for harvest!"

Read the story twice. Pause 2 seconds after each sequence word (First, Second, Third, Last).

3. Marking Rules & Partial Credit Guidelines

  • A1, C1, E1: 1 mark each. Strictly objective. If two choices are circled or unclear, award 0.
  • A2: 1 mark per correct box. If 4, 3, 2, 1 are in correct position, award 4 marks.
  • B1, B2: 1 mark each. Minor phonetic spelling slips (e.g., harvist, helmit, weav) receive full credit if clearly recognizable.
  • C2: 2 marks = perfectly ordered sentence. 1 mark = one word misplaced (e.g., We thankful are for the food). 0 marks = random order.
  • D1: 1 mark each for correct `-st` ending written legibly.
  • E2: 1 mark each. Single-word answers (`Mars`, `air`) are fully acceptable at CEFR A1.
  • F1: 2 marks = grammatically complete sentence with I am thankful for [noun]. 1 mark = phrase only (e.g., thankful my mom). 0 marks = blank.
  • F2: 2 marks = accurate There is [singular] or There are [plural] about the crops or farm. 1 mark = subject-verb agreement error (e.g., There is potatoes). 0 marks = blank or off-topic.
  • F3: 1 mark = capital letter at the start and full stop at the end of both sentences.
  • Handwriting: Do not penalize awkward letter formation if legible.

5. Speaking Rubric (12 Marks Total · 2 Minutes per Pupil)

Assess one-to-one or in groups of 3 while the rest of the class completes the quiet harvest festival card craft.

Station & Task4 Marks (Advanced)3 Marks (Secure)2 Marks (Developing)1 Mark (Beginning)
G1: Picture & Vocab
Teacher shows crop/festival card: "What do you see?"		 Names item immediately with full sentence: "I see pink potatoes / a full moon." Clear pronunciation. Names item with phrase: "Pink potatoes" or "Songpyeon" with good pronunciation. Names item after teacher hint or with non-standard pronunciation. Recognizes word only after a home-language prompt or isolated 1-word whisper.
G2: Setting Comprehension
"Where are Ruth and August? Why do they need helmets?"		 Answers both questions accurately: "On Mars. Because there is no air outside." Identifies Mars clearly; gives simple reason ("No air" or "To breathe"). Identifies setting ("Mars") but cannot explain helmet need. Requires heavy prompting to recall the planet or setting.
G3: Connected Speech
"Tell me: What are you thankful for?"		 Produces complete, natural sentence: "I am thankful for my family because they love me." Produces target frame: "I am thankful for my friends / food." Produces partial frame: "Thankful for mom" or uses a home-language word. Single isolated word ("Mom") with no sentence frame.
