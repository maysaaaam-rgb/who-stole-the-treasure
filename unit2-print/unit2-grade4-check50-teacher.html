Teacher Guide: English Progress Check · Grade 4 · Unit 2

Pupil paper: unit2-grade4-check50.html · Content: Global Readings 3 Unit 2: "What Lives Here?" (pp. 24–39).

Written Paper: 38 marks, about 38 minutes (A: 8 · B: 8 · C: 6 · D: 5 · E: 6 · F: 5). Speaking: 12 marks, 2 minutes per pupil in small groups. Total: 50 marks.

1. Listening Script (Read each item clearly TWICE at natural speed)

Say: "Section A. Listening. Put your finger on A1. Listen and circle the picture. Number 1."

A1.1: "Pine trees in the coniferous forest have green needles and cones." (pause 5 seconds, read again)

A1.2: "When the snow falls, the big brown bear hibernates in its warm den." (pause 5 seconds, read again)

A1.3: "Every spring, a gentle swallow builds its nest under the wooden roof." (pause 5 seconds, read again)

A1.4: "Listen to the heavy autumn rain falling on the leaves." (pause 5 seconds, read again)

Say: "Now look at A2. Listen to the story of Go Ne and the swallow. Number the pictures 1, 2, 3, 4."

A2 Story: "First, a tiny baby swallow fell from its nest onto the hard ground. Go Ne put it back safely. Second, the mother swallow returned and gave Go Ne a magical seed that grew into a giant fruit full of gold coins. Third, jealous Wen Shu hurt another bird, but Go Ne quickly rushed the injured swallow to the vet on his bicycle. Last, Wen Shu felt sorry and left a special roof-repair gift for Go Ne. They became true friends."

Read the story twice. Pause 2 seconds after each sequence word (First, Second, Third, Last).

3. Marking Rules & Partial Credit Guidelines

  • A1, C1, E1: 1 mark each. Strictly objective. If two choices are circled or unclear, award 0.
  • A2: 1 mark per correct box. If 4, 3, 2, 1 are in correct order, award 4 marks.
  • B1, B2: 1 mark each. Minor phonetic spelling slips (e.g., decidous, coniferus, nedles) receive full credit if clearly recognizable.
  • C2: 2 marks = perfectly ordered sentence. 1 mark = one word misplaced (e.g., Animals adapt they so can survive). 0 marks = random order.
  • D1: 1 mark each for correct `ai` letters written legibly.
  • E2: 1 mark each. For E2.5 accept "To inform" or "inform". For E2.6 accept "Coniferous" (capitalization not penalized).
  • F1: 2 marks = grammatically complete sentence with so it/they can + verb. 1 mark = missing subject or using infinitive (e.g., so can hide or so it can to hide). 0 marks = blank.
  • F2: 2 marks = accurate comparison using instead of. 1 mark = word order inverted but meaning clear. 0 marks = blank or off-topic.
  • F3: 1 mark = capital letter at the start and full stop at the end of both sentences.
  • Handwriting: Do not penalize handwriting if readable.

5. Speaking Rubric (12 Marks Total · 2 Minutes per Pupil)

Assess one-to-one or in groups of 3 while the rest of the class completes the quiet Forest Biome Diorama sketch.

Station & Task4 Marks (Advanced)3 Marks (Secure)2 Marks (Developing)1 Mark (Beginning)
G1: Forest Biome Description
Teacher shows forest picture: "Look at this picture. What kind of forest is it? What can you see?"		 Identifies forest accurately ("It is a coniferous forest") and describes 2 features: "There is snow and the trees have green needles." Identifies forest type and names 1 feature: "Coniferous forest with pine cones." Names 1–2 isolated words: "Trees", "Snow", but cannot name the forest category. Requires heavy home-language prompting to identify any tree or animal.
G2: Folk Tale Moral Comprehension
"Why did Wen Shu feel sorry? What did he do at the end?"		 Explains both motive and action: "He felt guilty because he hurt the swallow. He gave a gift to fix the roof." Explains the action: "He fixed the roof" or "He bought a gift for Go Ne." Gives partial 1-word answer: "Gift" or "Roof". Unable to recall story ending without teacher giving the answer.
G3: Connected Speech
"Why do hares have white fur in winter?"		 Produces full target formula: "Hares have white fur so they can hide in the snow." Fluent delivery. Produces target frame: "So they can hide" or "So they can survive." Produces incomplete frame: "To hide" or "Because white snow." Single isolated word: "Snow" with no clause connector.
