Late for the Show
Harry and his friends have a show at six o’clock. Harry plays the tree in the play. On the way, Harry’s car is stuck in traffic. “We are never going to get there!” says Harry. Then the train stops, and everybody waits on the platform. Lynn has an idea: “Why don’t we perform the show here?” The children perform the play on the platform, and everybody claps. Mr. Williams says, “Great teamwork!”
