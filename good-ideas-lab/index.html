Quest 1: The Rolling Pencil Puzzle & Nature's Secrets

+3 Badges Available

The Problem: When a round pencil is placed on a tilted desk, it rolls off and drops onto the floor! Which cross-section shape will give it a solution so it won't roll?

👨‍🔬

Thomas Edison's Inventor Rule:

"When you have a problem, look for a solution! I filled thousands of notebooks with ideas. An ordinary round pencil rolls, but a hexagon has 6 flat sides that lock onto the table!"

Quest 2: Leonardo's Blueprint Workshop

Badge: Parachute 🪂

Leonardo's Concept: "Drawing helps you see your idea and change it before building."
Adjust the blueprint canopy diameter using the slider. If the parachute is too small, air resistance cannot slow gravity down. If it is large enough, the inventor will float down gently!

📐 Blueprint Specifications

💡 Air Resistance Rule: A wider cloth canopy traps more air underneath, creating upward drag to counteract downward gravity!

Quest 3: The Iteration Loop with Karl Benz

Badge: Improve 🔧

The Golden Rule of Inventions: "Don't give up! Try again... and again... and again! Show your idea and listen to feedback."
Karl Benz built the world's first gas-powered motorcar in 1885. At first, it sputtered, shook violently, and tilted. Listen to the crowd and select engineering improvements!

🎩
Passerby 1:

"A carriage with no horses? It will never work!"

🧐
Passerby 2:

"Only three wheels? It shakes and tilts too easily!"

Step 1: Test Initial Model

Single-cylinder, 3 thin wooden wheels. Run the motor!

Step 2: Engine Counterbalance

Stabilize pistons and refine fuel mixture to stop stalling.

Step 3: 4th Wheel & Steering

Add a 4th wheel, wire-spoke balance & rack steering!
