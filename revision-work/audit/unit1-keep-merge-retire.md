## 2. Recommended Top 5 for Grade 3 Revision

1. **`clara-inventor` (Clara's Invention Mystery)**  
   *Focus:* Fiction reading comprehension (*The After-School Inventor*), story details (umbrella pen, alarm pillow, smart eraser, mirror, clean-up machine), and sequencing words (*First, Second, Third, Last*).
2. **`inventor-lab` (Inventor Lab: What Does It Take?)**  
   *Focus:* See-Think-Wonder visual inquiry on textbook pages 8–9, identifying everyday problems, and mastering the 5-step invention loop.
3. **`inventor-forge` (Inventor's Forge: The Apprentice Quest)**  
   *Focus:* Historical CLIL biographies (Thomas Edison, Leonardo da Vinci, Karl Benz, Wright Brothers), biomimicry from nature, and delivering a 60-second invention pitch.
4. **`young-inventor` (Young Inventors: Plan • Build • Test • Improve)**  
   *Focus:* Purpose sentence grammar (*People invented X to [verb]*), hands-on paper airplane / pinwheel engineering, and team challenge battles.
5. **`phonics` (Phonics Adventure: Blends & Digraphs)**  
   *Focus:* Phonics review isolating long vowel rhymes and high-stakes initial blends/digraphs (*sh, ch, th, st, pl, fl, cl*), essential for Turkish phonological calibration.

## 3. Recommended Top 5 for Grade 4 Revision

1. **`brain` (The Day Your Brain Quit!)**  
   *Focus:* Core reading strategy of skimming (*headings, pictures, bold keywords in 30 seconds*) and the 4 primary brain jobs (*Think, Learn, Remember, Imagine*).
2. **`alice-party-home` (Alice Tea Party Home Practice & Speaking Stage)**  
   *Focus:* Scaffolded speaking fluency using 3-sentence identity frames, 6 concrete picture words, and structured 1-minute pair conversations.
3. **`freeze-game` (The 20% Energy Freeze Game)**  
   *Focus:* Kinesthetic total physical response (TPR), comparing resting muscle energy with active 20% brain energy consumption, and linking senses to the 4 cerebral lobes.
4. **`brain-explorers` (Brain Explorers: How Your Brain Reads)**  
   *Focus:* CLIL neuroscience connecting reading mechanics across the brain (*Occipital = seeing letters, Temporal = matching phonics, Parietal = line tracking, Frontal = comprehension*).
5. **`alice-quest` (Alice in Wonderland XP Quest Deck)**  
   *Focus:* Tiered differentiated reading comprehension, character motivation analysis, and self-directed student quest selection.
