# Turkish Learner Pedagogical Notes: Unit 1
> **Student Demographics:** Turkish Primary ESL Learners (Grade 3 & Grade 4, Classes 3A, 3B, 4A, 4B)  
> **L1 Influence:** Turkish (Agglutinative, SOV word order, vowel harmony, no grammatical gender, no definite article, no verb "to have", restricted consonant clusters).

### 1.2 Top 10 Turkish-L1 Mistakes & 1-Line Teacher Fixes (Grade 3)

1. **Pronoun Gender Confusion (O = He/She/It):**  
   *Student says:* "Clara is an inventor. He made a clean-up machine."  
   *One-Line Teacher Fix:* Point to the girl mascot and chant: "Girl = SHE 👧, Boy = HE 👦, Machine = IT ⚙️!"
2. **Initial /s/-Cluster Prothesis (*s- consonant*):**  
   *Student says:* "Clara is very *ismart* and made a *ispecial* mirror."  
   *One-Line Teacher Fix:* Teach the "Snake Hiss" without any /i/ sound: "Hiss like a snake first: *ssss-mart*, *ssss-pecial*!"
3. **Missing Definite Article (*The*):**  
   *Student says:* "Press button on mirror to see back."  
   *One-Line Teacher Fix:* Use the "Hold Hands" rule: "When we talk about one special thing, put **THE** in front: *the button*, *the mirror*!"
4. **Numeral + Plural -s Conflict (*iki kitap*, not *iki kitaplar*):**  
   *Student says:* "Karl Benz built a car with three wheel."  
   *One-Line Teacher Fix:* Draw a buzzing snake tail on the board: "Number bigger than 1 needs a buzzing **-S** tail: *three wheelSSS*!"
5. **Verb "Have Got / Has Got" vs "To Be":**  
   *Student says:* "My invention is three buttons." or "I am have a pencil."  
   *One-Line Teacher Fix:* Pat your pocket for possession: "Say: 'I **have got** a pencil' / 'It **has got** three buttons'!"
6. **Final Stop Deletion in Consonant Clusters (-st, -nt, -ct):**  
   *Student says:* "Firss, have an idea. Nex, make a plan. Las, tes the machine."  
   *One-Line Teacher Fix:* Tap the desk firmly on the final consonant click: "Firs-**T**! Nex-**T**! Las-**T**! Tes-**T**!"
7. **SOV Word Order in Purpose Clauses:**  
   *Student says:* "People cars to quickly travel invented."  
   *One-Line Teacher Fix:* Use the Train Blocks visual: `[People invented] -> [cars] -> [TO travel quickly]`.
8. **Confusion Between /w/ and /v/:**  
   *Student says:* "The alarm clock pillow *vakes* Clara with a song."  
   *One-Line Teacher Fix:* Shape lips into an 'O' for W: "Round your lips like blowing a candle: *W-W-Wake*, not teeth-on-lip *V*!"
9. **Dental Fricative /θ/ Substituted with /t/ or /s/:**  
   *Student says:* "First, second, *tird* step." or "I *tink* it is good."  
   *One-Line Teacher Fix:* "Bite your tongue sandwich: put tongue between your teeth and blow: *TH-TH-Third*, *TH-TH-Think*!"
10. **Present Simple 3rd-Person -s Dropping:**  
    *Student says:* "Clara invent things after school."  
    *One-Line Teacher Fix:* Give the singular person an extra 'S' badge: "One person (Clara) gives the verb an **S**: Clara invent**S**!"

### 1.3 6 Phonics Minimal Pairs for Grade 3 Targets

1. **/w/ vs /v/:** `wet` (rain) vs `vet` (animal doctor) — *Stops students saying "vet homework".*
2. **/θ/ vs /t/:** `think` (have an idea) vs `tink` / `tin` (metal can) — *Bite tongue sandwich vs tap roof of mouth.*
3. **/ʃ/ vs /tʃ/:** `ship` (boat) vs `chip` (computer part) — *Quiet librarian /ʃ/ vs sneezing train /tʃ/.*
4. **/cl/ vs /gl/:** `clean` (tidy room) vs `glean` (gather) / `clap` vs `glad` — *Voiceless throat burst for Clara's clean-up.*
5. **/st/ vs /s/:** `fast` (quick travel) vs `pass` (go by) — *Crisp /st/ explosion at word end.*
6. **/fl/ vs /pl/:** `fly` (travel in sky) vs `ply` / `plan` (drawing steps) — *Bite lower lip for /fl/ vs pop two lips for /pl/.*

### 2.2 Top 10 Turkish-L1 Mistakes & 1-Line Teacher Fixes (Grade 4)

1. **WH-Question Inversion & Auxiliary Dropping:**  
   *Student says:* "Where the White Rabbit went?" or "Why Alice fell?"  
   *One-Line Teacher Fix:* The Question Helper rule: "Question word + **DID** + Person + Verb: *Where **did** the rabbit go?*"
2. **Silent Letter Phonetic Reading (Cupboard = /kup-board/):**  
   *Student says:* "Alice saw *kup-board* on the wall."  
   *One-Line Teacher Fix:* Strike out the 'P': "The 'P' is sleeping! Say: **CUB-berd**!"
3. **Past Simple Regular -ed Pronounced as Extra Syllable /-ed/:**  
   *Student says:* "Alice *chase-ed* the rabbit and *look-ed* at the shelf."  
   *One-Line Teacher Fix:* Teach the Stop T sound: "After 's' or 'k', -ed sounds like a fast **T**: *chast!*, *lookt!*"
4. **Agent / Comparative Suffix Confusion (-er):**  
   *Student says:* "He is a good read." or "Reading makes brain fast-er-er."  
   *One-Line Teacher Fix:* "Add **-er** to person who does the job: read -> read**ER**, learn -> learn**ER**!"
5. **Adjective Confusion: -ed vs -ing (Bored vs Boring):**  
   *Student says:* "Alice was very boring sitting by the river."  
   *One-Line Teacher Fix:* Touch your heart for feelings: "-ED is how **YOU FEEL** (I am bored 🥱); -ING is the **THING** (The book is boring 📖)!"
6. **Preposition Confusion (Under / In / Down):**  
   *Student says:* "Alice fell under the rabbit hole."  
   *One-Line Teacher Fix:* Gesture downward: "Use **DOWN** for falling through a deep tunnel: Alice fell **DOWN** the hole!"
7. **Percentage & Number Syntax:**  
   *Student says:* "The brain uses percentage twenty energy." (Turkish: *yüzde yirmi*).  
   *One-Line Teacher Fix:* Flip to English order: "Say number first, percent second: **20 percent (20%)**, not percent twenty!"
8. **Vest vs Waistcoat Confusion:**  
   *Student says:* "White Rabbit wears a west."  
   *One-Line Teacher Fix:* "Bite your lip for V: **Vest** in America; **Waistcoat** in Britain!"
9. **Missing Plural on Irregular Nouns (Shelves / Knives):**  
   *Student says:* "She saw many books on the *shelfs*."  
   *One-Line Teacher Fix:* The V transformation: "One shelf -> Many shel**VES**!"
10. **Locative "In My Brain" vs Subject "My Brain":**  
    *Student says:* "In my brain learns English." (Turkish: *Beynimde öğreniyor*).  
    *One-Line Teacher Fix:* Put the actor in front: "**My brain** learns English! The brain is the boss!"

---

### 2.3 6 Phonics Minimal Pairs for Grade 4 Targets

1. **/w/ vs /v/:** `vest` (US waistcoat) vs `west` (direction) — *Teeth on lower lip /v/ vs rounded whistle lips /w/.*
2. **/nd/ vs /n/:** `land` (ground) vs `pan` / `ban` — *Crisp dental /d/ latch on the final cluster.*
3. **/ŋ/ vs /ŋk/:** `wing` (bird) vs `wink` (eye blink) — *Nasal vocal chime /ŋ/ vs explosive back stop /ŋk/.*
4. **/st/ vs /s/:** `past` (yesterday) vs `pass` (ticket) — *Keep the -st brake sharp.*
5. **/tr/ vs /tʃr/:** `tree` (plant) vs `three` (number) — *Clean alveolar stop /tr/ vs interdental fricative /θr/.*
6. **/ɜː(r)/ vs /ɔː(r)/:** `learn` / `word` vs `lawn` / `ward` — *Central open vowel in learner / thinker / worker.*
