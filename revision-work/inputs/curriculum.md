## GRADE 3: Global Readings 2
### Phonics Review (pp 4-7): long vowels a_e, ee/ea, i_e, o_e, u_e; blends and digraphs sh, st, ch, pl, fl, pr, fr
Words: cake, lake, tape, rain, tree, leaf, bike, kite, rope, nose, bone, cube, tube, flute, ship, shop, fish, bench, chips, star, nest, flag, frog
### Unit 1: What Does It Do? (pp 8-23), 4 weeks, 8 lessons. Content: Science/Technology. Reading skill: identifying a sequence of events. SEL: self-awareness.
Reading 1 story: The After-School Inventor (Clara Doodle, cl- blend). Reading 2 instructional text: My Good Ideas Book (Edison, da Vinci, biomimicry: burrs to Velcro, kingfisher to bullet train).
Target vocab: invent, try again, change, mirror, mess, invention, machine, problem, solution, hexagon, improve, parachute, plan, eraser, tidy
Lessons: 1 See-Think-Wonder (everyday inventions; Why do people invent things?); 2 Reading 1 aloud, track Clara's 4 inventions, cl- blend; 3 words in context, action verbs, American vs British vocabulary; 4 comprehension (match inventions to functions, 6 recall/inference questions, debate); 5 sequence words First, Second, Third, Last; 6 Reading 2 informational text; 7 problem solving, 5-step engineering feedback loop; 8 Think Together, match inventors to inventions, Reading Journal.
### Unit 2: Thank You (pp 24-39). Content: Social Studies. Reading skill: understanding setting.
Reading 1: Chuseok, Korea's Harvest Festival. Reading 2: First Harvest (-st blend). Vocab: harvest, festival, celebrate, tradition, feast, gratitude, first, crops. (NOT yet taught: this is the upcoming unit.)

## GRADE 4: Global Readings 3
### Literacy Review (pp 4-7): Don't Move (fiction, final -st, initial tr-), The Monarch Butterfly's Journey (nonfiction, final -nd, -ng). Skills: scanning, cause and effect, sequence.
Vocab: freeze, still, branch, predator, journey, migration, monarch, chrysalis, caterpillar, route, travel
### Unit 1: I Love Reading (pp 8-23), 8 lessons. Content: Science/Neuroscience. Reading skill: skimming a text (headings, first sentences, visuals).
Reading 1: The Beginning of Alice's Adventures (Lewis Carroll). Reading 2: Learning and Your Brain (neurons, synapses, lobes).
Target vocab: sleepy, chased, fell, shelves, corner, vest/waistcoat, lobes, workout, cells, fibers, messages, connecting, creative, visualize
Lessons: 1 See-Think-Wonder (Why read?); 2 question starts Who/What/Where/When/Why/How; 3 Reading 1 Alice part 1; 4 words in context and British English; 5 chronological order and feelings (boredom to astonishment); 6 skimming; 7 Reading 2 brain; 8 brain facts, final -er spelling (agent nouns, comparatives), Reading Journal.
### Unit 2: What Lives Here? (pp 24-39). Content: Ecology. Reading skill: cause and effect.
Reading 1: The Secret Burrow (animal homes). Reading 2: Ecosystems in Balance (food webs). Vocab: habitat, burrow, ecosystem, predator, prey, shelter, balance, organism. (NOT yet taught: upcoming.)
