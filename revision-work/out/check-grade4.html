E. Reading 📖 6 marks

🧠 Your Brain Loves Reading

Brain cells

Your brain has many cells. These cells send messages to each other. When you read, the cells make new connections. Reading is like a workout for your brain!

Parts of the brain

Your brain has different parts. They are called lobes. One lobe helps you see the words. Another lobe helps you understand them.

Pictures in your head

When you read a story, you can visualize it. You make pictures in your head. This helps you to be creative.
