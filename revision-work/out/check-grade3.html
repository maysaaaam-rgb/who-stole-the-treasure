E. Reading 📖 6 marks

Mia's Good Idea

Mia has a problem. Her room is a mess. There are books and toys on the floor.

First, Mia makes a plan. Second, she builds a machine. Third, she tests the machine. Oh no! It doesn't work.

Mia doesn't stop. She changes the machine and tries again.

Last, the machine works! It picks up the toys. Now Mia's room is tidy. Mia is happy.
