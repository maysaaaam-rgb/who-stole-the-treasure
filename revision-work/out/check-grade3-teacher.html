Teacher Guide: English Progress Check · Grade 3 · Term 1

Pupil paper: check-grade3.html · Content: Global Readings 2 Phonics Review, Unit 1 "What Does It Do?", Monster Day assignment (taught to 3A and 3B). No Unit 2 content.

Paper: 38 marks, about 38 minutes (A 8 · B 8 · C 6 · D 5 · E 6 · F 5). Speaking: 12 marks, 1–2 minutes per pupil, done one to one while the class does a quiet task. Total 50.

1. Listening script (read each part TWICE)

Say: "Section A. Listen. Circle the picture. Number 1."

1. "I can see a parachute." (pause 5 seconds, read again)

2. "My monster has got big claws."

3. "Look at the frog."

4. "This is a kite."

Say: "Now A2. Listen to the story. Number the pictures 1, 2, 3, 4."

"Clara has a problem. First, she makes a plan. Second, she builds a machine. Third, she tests the machine. It doesn't work, so she changes it. Last, she tries again and the machine works! Hooray!"

Read the A2 story twice at a slow, natural speed. Pause 2 seconds after each sequence word.

3. Marking rules (partial credit)

  • A1, C1, D1, E1: 1 mark each. If two answers are circled, give 0.
  • A2: 1 mark for each picture in the correct position (so 2 correct = 2 marks).
  • B1, B2: 1 mark each. Accept small copying errors if the word is clearly the right one (e.g. clow for claw). For B1 also accept tooth for 🦷 and eyes for 👁️. Capital letters are not needed.
  • C2: 2 = all words in the correct order. 1 = one word out of place or missing. 0 = more errors. Capital and full stop are not needed here.
  • E2: 1 mark each. Short answers and spelling errors are fine if the meaning is clear (machin = 1).
  • F1: 2 = complete sentence with has got + body part (number or colour is a bonus, not required). 1 = meaning is clear but there is a grammar error (e.g. have got eye) or only a phrase. 0 = blank or not about a monster.
  • F2: 2 = names the invention and says what it does with a verb (It cleans…). 1 = only one part, or the verb has no -s. 0 = blank or unrelated.
  • F3: 1 = both sentences start with a capital letter and end with a full stop. 0 = otherwise.
  • Never take marks off for messy handwriting if you can read it.
