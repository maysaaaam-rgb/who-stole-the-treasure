📘 How to Run the Thu–Fri Units 1–5 Speaking Quiz (35 Min)

1. Warm-Up & Model (0–5 min)

Tell the class: “You will talk with me for 1 minute — 5 tiny picture questions, one from each unit. Mistakes are totally OK!” Model one prompt with a confident volunteer.

2. Pair Practice Cards (5–10 min)

Hand out the printed Practice Cards (not the exact quiz prompts). Pairs take turns asking and answering in full sentences while you call pupils 1-by-1 to your desk.

3. 1-on-1 Speaking Quiz (10–32 min)

Each pupil automatically gets randomized question versions across Units 1–5 so nobody can copy answers in line. Smile, wait 5 seconds before showing a hint, and tap 0, 1, or 2.

4. Bands & Celebration (32–35 min)

0–2: Below A1 · 3–5: A1 · 6–8: A1+ · 9–10: A1+ Strong. Tap ⭐ Give XP after each pupil, then finish with a 3D Speaking Game celebration!
