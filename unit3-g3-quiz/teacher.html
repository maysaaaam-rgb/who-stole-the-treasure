Unit 3 Quiz: Teacher Page

Listening script, answers for Form A and Form B, how to mark

Listening script (read each part twice, slowly)

  1. A young man fished through a hole in the ice. One day, a giant polar bear took his fish.
  2. The winters were warm, so the ice was thin. The man and the bear fell into the water.
  3. Gasoline cars are fast, but they are noisy. They make the air dirty.
  4. Electric cars and solar cars are both clean and quiet.

Marking

  • Total 100: Game 1 = 15, Game 2 = 20, Game 3 = 15, Listening = 12, Compare = 18, Summary = 10, Speaking = 10. Bonus +5 (any sensible “I can ... because ...”).
  • Snake: 1 point for each of the 8 words on the line, +2 for writing the 4 picture words (BEAR, HOLE, TRAP, MELTING). Touching a word that is not from Unit 3 = no point for that word.
  • Give Form A and Form B to neighbours: the maze exit, the snake path, the order of the answers and the clue letters are different.
  • Speaking: stamp 10 (clear and correct), 7 (small mistakes), 4 (needs help). It is never a punishment: let the pupil try again.
