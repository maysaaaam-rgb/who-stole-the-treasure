4 · Photo frame (put your head in!)

12 pages that make one big frame. The first page is the map. Tape the pages together, glue them onto cardboard and cut out the middle.

Photo frame · how to put it together

A1A2A3A4B1openingB4C1C4D1D2D3D4
  1. Print the 12 frame pages (A4, scale 100%).
  2. Cut off the white edge on the sides that join another page.
  3. Lay them out like the map: A1 top left, D4 bottom right. The middle stays empty.
  4. Tape them together on the back, then glue them onto a big piece of cardboard and cut out the opening.

Finished size about 76 × 111 cm. The opening is about 38 × 55 cm: big enough for a head and shoulders.
