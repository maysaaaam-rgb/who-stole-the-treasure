🪜 Our 6 stages

If we forget a rule, we go up one stage. 1 is first, 6 is last. A new day is a new start.

1
🟡
WarningMy teacher tells me quietly. I stop.Uyarı
2
🟠
Move seatI sit in another seat until the bell.Yer değiştirme
3
🔴
No gamesI watch and write. I do not play today.Bugün oyun yok
4
✉️
Message to my parentsMy teacher writes to my family today.Veliye mesaj
5
⚽
No sport lessonI miss one sport lesson. I sit and watch.Bir beden eğitimi dersi yok
6
⚫
Out of the lessonI go to my class teacher. We make a plan.Dersten çıkış
🙋 I can always ask for help: “Can I have a break?” · “Can you help me?”
