⏱️ 1. 25-MINUTE CLASSROOM PACING PLAN Zero Stress
• Mins 0–5 (5 mins) — Skill 1: Listening & -ar Phonics (Page 1 Top): Read the 6 short prompts below (or click "🔊 Play All Listening" on the Smartboard). Students circle picture A or B and write the -ar word.
• Mins 5–12 (7 mins) — Skill 2: Reading Detective (Page 1 Bottom): Students bust Bubbles's 4 Fake-News cards (True/Lie + circle the fix) and match the 3 Problem & Solution Dominoes.
• Mins 12–18 (6 mins) — Skill 3: Creative Writing (Page 2 Top): Students fill in their Saving Jar, Donating Jar, 2099 Money Invention (+ quick 10-sec doodle!), and 2 Comic Strip bubbles.
• Mins 18–25 (7 mins) — Skill 4: Speaking Board Game (Page 2 Bottom): Pairs play Rock-Paper-Scissors at their desks! As they land on Spaces 1–6, they ask and answer out loud while you walk around, listen, and stamp their sheets!
🎙️ 2. TEACHER LISTENING SCRIPT (Read each item twice, or press "🔊 Play All Listening" on Smartboard) 6 Marks

A1.1 (6000 BCE): "Thousands of years ago, in 6000 BCE, people didn't use coins. When a farmer wanted a new goat, he bartered for it with bags of grain! Circle A (grain and goat) and write bartered."

A1.2 (1200 BCE): "In 1200 BCE, carrying heavy goats was too hard! So people started using light cowrie shells as money. Circle B and write hard."

A1.3 (1958 CE): "In 1958, people didn't have to carry paper money or metal knives. Instead, they paid with a small plastic credit card! Circle A and write card."

A1.4 (The $100 Bill): "After school, Paige walked through the autumn park on her way home and saw a hundred-dollar bill in the leaves! Circle A and write park."

A2.5 (Quote 1): (In a teasing, laughing voice) "Oh, Paige! You don't have a cell phone? You really should get one! Ha ha ha!" — Who said that? Tick Jodie!

A2.6 (Quote 2): (In a tired, gentle fatherly voice) "Oh, Paige, I'd love to go to a restaurant, but you know money is tight right now." — Who said that? Tick Dad!
