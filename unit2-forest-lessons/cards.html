Lesson 2 · The Roof Mystery · witness cards

Groups of six. Each pupil gets ONE card. Read your clue out loud. Do not show your card! The group decides: who left the gift on the roof?
Witness 1
“It was not an animal.”
Witness 2
“It was not a grown-up.”
Witness 3
“It was not the boy from that house.”
Witness 4
“He hit a swallow with his slingshot.”
Witness 5
“He watched Go Ne pay the vet.”
Witness 6
“He felt sorry. He left a gift and went home.”
Suspects: 👨 Go Ne's father · 🧑‍⚕️ the vet · 🧒 Wen Shu · 👦 Go Ne · 🐦 the mother swallow · 🐤 the baby bird
