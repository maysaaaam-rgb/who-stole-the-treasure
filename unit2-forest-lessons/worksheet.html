D. Accept any true 3-sentence retell. 1 mark for each sentence that starts with First, Then, At the end and has a past verb. Example: First, Go Ne helped a baby bird. Then, the swallow gave him a gold seed. At the end, Wen Shu said sorry and they became friends.

Teacher notes

Common mistakes: falled, growed, payed (see the Verb Vault); instead without of; because used where so they can is needed.
