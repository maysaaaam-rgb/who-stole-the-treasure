Teacher Copy

Part 1: Time Capsule Objects & Message

6 Correct Capsule Objects: 1795 Coins, Newspapers, A Letter, A Photo, A Toy, Gold Disc (Do not circle: Huge Chariot, Live Pharaoh).

Sample Message: “Hello, people of the future! I want you to know about our school and animals. And books, movies, and music. From, [Pupil’s Name].”

Part 2: Word Search (10×10 Grid Locations)

1. FUTURE: Row 1, Cols 1–6  |  2. OBJECTS: Row 2, Cols 1–7  |  3. COINS: Row 3, Cols 1–5  |  4. EARTH: Row 4, Cols 1–5  |  5. DISC: Row 5, Cols 1–4  |  6. WIND: Row 6, Cols 1–4  |  7. PAST: Row 7, Cols 1–4  |  8. ANCIENT: Row 9, Cols 1–7 (Bonus: Row 8 SPACE, Row 10 VOYAGER).

Part 3: Sentence Scramble

  1. This is a time capsule.
  2. People lived in the past.
  3. I want to see the future.
  4. It had coins and newspapers.
  5. Each spacecraft has a gold disc.

Part 4: Word Sorting (3 Columns)

PLACES 🌍: Earth, ancient city, school
SOUNDS 🔊: wind, thunder, music
THINGS WE CAN TOUCH ✋: coins, a toy, a letter

Part 5: Opposites Matching

1. E (past–future)  |  2. C (old–new)  |  3. F (ancient–modern)  |  4. G (big–small)  |  5. A (loud–quiet)  |  6. H (happy–sad)  |  7. D (open–close)  |  8. B (day–night)
