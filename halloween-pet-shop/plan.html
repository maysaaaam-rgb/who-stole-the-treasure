🏥 Monster Pet Vet Clinic (CLIL Health & PPP Method)

Grade 3 (A1) · 35 minutes · CLIL Veterinary Science & Health Care · PPP (Presentation → Practice → Production) · 4–5 teams

🎯 Targeted Outcomes (Students will be able to…)
  • A1 Symptom & State Identification: Ask "What's the matter?" and express feelings & aches (It is tired/hungry/cold. It has got a tummy ache / sore throat / hurt wing).
  • A1 Care Commands: Prescribe simple care using imperatives (Give it warm water and a blanket! Put a bandage on its paw!).
  • A1+ Symptom & Cause Diagnosis: Explain illnesses and their causes using because + Past Simple (It has got a stomach ache because it ate ten sweets).
  • A1+ Medical Advice: Give positive and negative care advice using should / shouldn't (It should drink warm honey tea. It shouldn't eat chocolate!).
  • CLIL Science Link: Connect symptoms → causes → healthy treatments in a collaborative Vet Team roleplay.
🧬 Methodology: CLIL + PPP
  • CLIL (Health & Animal Welfare): Pupils act as Monster Vets checking temperature, diagnosing causes (diet, weather, rest, injury), and prescribing real care.
  • P1 · Presentation (11 min): Station 1 X-Ray Scanner (receptive listening deduction) + Station 2 Vet Check-Up Table (meaning, colour-coded form, choral pronunciation & TPR).
  • P2 · Practice (6 min): Station 3 Medicine Cabinet Match (controlled team game matching symptoms/causes to prescriptions).
  • P3 · Production (18 min): Station 4 Team Vet Report Card prep (8 min) + Station 5 Live Vet Clinic Roleplay, Health Inspector Q&A & Healing Hearts (10 min).
🖨️ Materials & Preparation
  • Print worksheet.html: 1 Vet Report Card per team (p. 1), 1 set of Health Inspector Question Cards per team (p. 2), Door Pass slips (p. 3).
  • Coloured pencils on every team table.
  • Open halloween-pet-shop/index.html and select the class.
🗣️ Target Language & Colour Code
  • Amber (Symptoms): tired, hungry, thirsty, cold, dizzy, tummy/stomach ache, sore throat, hurt wing/paw
  • Cyan (Cause/Body): has got, wing, paw, legs · because it ate sweets / flew in the rain / shouted / danced
  • Green (Treatment): Give it warm water / honey tea / milk / a blanket / a bandageIt should drink tea / rest · It shouldn't eat sweets / fly

PPP Lesson Stages (35 Minutes)

TimePPP Stage & StationTeacher ActionPupil & Team Action
5 minP1 · Presentation A
1 · Vet X-Ray Scanner		Play Clue 1 (symptom). Let teams whisper. Reveal Clue 2 (cause) & Clue 3 (cure). Tap the patient the team chooses.Listen to the 3 clinical clues, deduce which patient is in the X-Ray Scanner (+3/+2/+1 pts).
6 minP1 · Presentation B
2 · CLIL Vet Check-Up		Open 4–5 patients. Highlight the colour code (Symptom → Cause → Cure). Press "🔁 Repeat", "🎭 Act it!", and run the 1-tap CLIL Quick Check.Choral repeat, act out the symptom & treatment (TPR), and answer the CLIL Quick Check (+1 pt).
6 minP2 · Practice
3 · Medicine Cabinet Match		Read the patient's chart on the Exam Table. Call the team on turn to choose the matching prescription from the 4 cabinet cards.Read the symptom & cause, select the correct Give it…should / shouldn't treatment (+2 pts), and read it aloud.
8 minP3 · Production Prep
4 · Team Vet Lab		Run the 30s Jobs Timer (Head Vet, X-Ray Artist, Medical Writer, Clinic Manager). Start the 7-min lab timer and circulate.Draw the patient & symptom on the Vet Report Card, write the diagnosis & prescription, and rehearse twice.
10 minP3 · Production
5 · Open the Vet Clinic!		Call each Vet Team (90s). Spin a Health Inspector Question for other teams to ask. Then run the 3 Healing Hearts (💚) vote & Door Pass.Present the patient report, answer 1 Inspector question, award 3 Healing Hearts, and say 1 sentence at the door.

Differentiation & Inclusion

Support (Emerging A1 Learners)
  • Assign X-Ray Artist or Clinic Manager roles first; they join the choral final line ("Now our pet is happy and healthy!").
  • Every chip on the board and worksheet has a clear emoji icon.
Challenge (Fast Finishers & Strong A1+)
  • Add a temperature reading and a 2nd shouldn't warning or a 3-day care schedule.
  • Act as Lead Health Inspector and ask 2 follow-up questions without looking at the card.
