🎃 Halloween Monster Pet Shop

Grade 3 · 35 minutes · teams of 4–5 (All About Me survey teams) · A1 · smartboard + 1 Pet Design Card per team

Pupils will…
  • describe a pet: It's a black cat. It has got a hat. It can climb.It's a small, black cat. It can climb, but it can't swim. It likes fish!
  • ask and answer: Can it fly? Has it got a tail? How much is it?What can it do? Is it friendly? What does it like?
  • do a team job and present together
Why this lesson (survey)
  • Top answers: cats (23), pets (40), drawing (40), pizza and burgers
  • Hardest: speaking in front of the class (20), finishing in time (21)
  • So: team speaking, a job for everyone, timers, pictures for every word
Prepare
  • Print (worksheet.html): 1 Pet Design Card per team, 1 set of question cards per team, door-pass slips, homework (optional)
  • Coloured pencils for every team
  • Open the lesson and tap the class before the pupils come in
Key words
  • colours: black, white, orange, purple, green, red
  • has got: big eyes, a long tail, wings, eight legs, a hat
  • can: fly, climb, jump, run, dance, swim, roll · can't · likes

Lesson steps

TimeStationTeacher doesPupils do
4 min1 · Who Is in the Cauldron?Read clue 1, then "Next clue". Tap the picture the team points at.Listen, whisper in the team, point. Guess early = more points.
6 min2 · Meet the PetsOpen 4–5 pets. "Say it with me", then "Act it!". Point at the colours (yellow = colour, blue = has got, green = can).Repeat together (choral), act the verb.
10 min3 · Team Pet Lab"⏱ 30 seconds" for jobs, hand out cards, start the 8-minute timer. Walk round: help Writers with frames, let Speakers practise in the team.Artist draws, Writer writes, Speaker practises, Timer Boss calls "2 minutes left!". Teams can use the Sentence Builder.
12 min4 · The Pet Shop Is Open!One team at a time (90 s). Give ⭐⭐ for clear sentences, 🤝 for teamwork, +1 for good buyer questions. Tick the Speaking Check (page 2). Then "Buy a pet!": 3 coins per team.Speaker presents, the team says the last line together, buyers ask 1 question each.
3 min5 · Awards & Door PassRead the award titles. Stand at the door.Say 1 sentence at the door (or point at a pet and say its name).

Support and challenge

Pupils who need more help
  • Give them the Artist or Timer Boss job first; they say only the team's last line
  • Door pass: point at a picture + say the pet's name
  • Sentence Builder words all have pictures
Fast finishers
  • Add a 4th sentence: It has got … and … / It likes …
  • Be the buyer who asks 2 questions
  • Draw a second pet on the back of the card

Soft-fail: no minus points for wrong answers; Luna gives a hint and the team tries again. Team points stay on the lesson page; give team XP on the Class Board after the lesson if you want.
Homework (optional): "My Pet at Home" (worksheet page 4): draw, 3 sentences, say them to the family.
