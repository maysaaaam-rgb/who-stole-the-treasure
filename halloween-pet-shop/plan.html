🏥 Monster Pet Vet Clinic (CLIL Health & 6-Step PPP Method)

Grade 3 (A1) · 35 minutes · CLIL Veterinary Science & Health Care · True PPP Flow (Presentation → Practice → Production)

🎯 Targeted Outcomes (Students will be able to…)
  • A1 Symptom & State Identification: Ask "What's the matter?" and express feelings & aches (It is tired/cold. It has got a tummy ache / sore throat / hurt paw).
  • A1 Care Imperatives: Prescribe simple care commands (Give it warm water and a blanket! Put a bandage on its paw!).
  • A1+ Symptom & Cause Diagnosis: Diagnose illnesses and causes using because + Past Simple (It has got a stomach ache because it ate ten sweets).
  • A1+ Medical Advice: Give positive and negative advice using should / shouldn't (It should drink warm honey tea. It shouldn't eat sweets!).
  • CLIL Science Link: Connect symptoms → causes → healthy treatments in a collaborative Vet Team roleplay.
🧬 True 6-Step PPP + CLIL Architecture
  • P1 · Presentation 1 (5 min): Teach the 3-Block Grammar Formula + 6 Flippable Symptom ↔ Cure Flashcards with TPR actions before any testing!
  • P1 · Presentation 2 (6 min): Inspect the 3-Box Medical Charts of the 7 patients + choral echo + 1-tap CLIL Check.
  • P2 · Practice 1 (5 min): Receptive deduction game inside the Digital Medical X-Ray Monitor.
  • P2 · Practice 2 (6 min): 2-Step Medicine Cabinet Match (+1 pt CLIL cure, +1 pt Grammar Fixer).
  • P3 · Production (13 min): Team Vet Card Studio (6 min) + Live Vet Clinic Presentations, Inspector Q&A & Healing Hearts (7 min).
🖨️ Printables (worksheet.html)
  • Page 1 (1 per team): Official Vet Report Card with Step 1 Tick-Boxes, Step 2 X-Ray Drawing Box, and Step 3 Labelled Fill-in Slots (with hints under every blank).
  • Page 2: Quick 2-min PPP Grammar Practice + Cut-Out Health Inspector Question Cards.
  • Pages 3–4: Door Pass Exit Tickets & Home Vet Check-Up Homework.
🗣️ Target Language & Colour Code
  • Amber (1 · Symptom): tired, hungry, cold, dizzy, tummy/stomach ache, sore throat, hurt paw/wing
  • Cyan (2 · Cause/Body): has got, paw, wing · because it ate sweets / flew in the rain / shouted / danced
  • Green (3 · Cure/Advice): Give it warm water / honey tea / a blanket / a bandageIt should drink tea / rest · It shouldn't eat sweets

6-Step PPP Lesson Stages (35 Minutes)

TimePPP Stage & StationTeacher ActionPupil & Team Action
5 minP1 · Presentation 1
1 · Symptoms, Cures & Rule		Tap the 3 Grammar Formula blocks at the top. Then tap each of the 6 Flashcards, press "🎭 Act!", and flip to the green Cure side.Listen, repeat chorally, do the TPR gesture for each symptom, and read the cure aloud.
6 minP1 · Presentation 2
2 · Meet the 7 Patients		Open 4–5 patients on the Waiting Bench. Walk through the 3-Box Medical Chart, press "🔁 Repeat", and run the Quick Check.Echo the 3 chart boxes together and answer the CLIL Quick Check (+1 pt).
5 minP2 · Practice 1
3 · Medical X-Ray Scanner		Reveal Clue 1 (Symptom), Clue 2 (Body/Cause), Clue 3 (Cure) on the X-Ray Monitor. Tap the patient the team selects.Listen to the clues, whisper in teams, and diagnose the mystery patient (+3/+2/+1 pts).
6 minP2 · Practice 2
4 · Medicine & Grammar Fixer		Run the 2-Step cases: let the team pick the cure in Step 1 (+1 pt), then tap the missing grammar keyword in Step 2 (+1 pt).Match the symptom to the cure, then complete has got / is / Givebecause / should / shouldn't.
6 minP3 · Production Prep
5 · Team Vet Card Studio		Run the 30s Jobs Timer, hand out Page 1, and start the 6-min timer. Use the 4 clean Studio tabs to model filling the card.Tick the symptom & cure, draw the patient, fill in the labelled boxes, and rehearse twice.
7 minP3 · Production
6 · Live Vet Clinic & Awards		Call each Vet Team (90s). Spin a Health Inspector Question for other teams to ask. Run the 3 Healing Hearts (💚) vote & Door Pass.Present the Vet Report, answer 1 Inspector question, vote with 3 Healing Hearts, and say 1 sentence at the door.
