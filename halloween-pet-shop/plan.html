⏱️ 35-Minute Whole-Class + Individual PPP Flow (No Group Chaos!)

PPP Stage & TimeSmartboard ScreenTeacher & Student Action (Whole-Class + 1-by-1 Turns)
1 · Presentation A
5 min		 6 Problems, 6 Cures & Grammar Rule Tap the 3-Block Grammar Rule at the top. Then tap each of the 6 Picture Flashcards: class repeats the 2-word problem (a tummy ache, a cold, a sore throat, a hurt paw, tired, hungry), does the 2-second mime (🎭 Act!), and taps 💊 Flip to Cure! to see the 2-word picture cure (warm water, a blanket, warm tea, a bandage, a soft bed, milk).
2 · Presentation B
6 min		 Meet the 6 Patients
(3 Short Lines)		 Tap 4–5 patients on the Waiting Bench. Each patient has only 3 short lines (5–6 words each!) with 3D pictures. Press 🔁 Echo 3 Lines for choral repetition, then call 1 student to tap the 2-word Picture Check at the bottom (+1 Star).
3 · Practice A
6 min		 Medical X-Ray Scanner
(1 Student at a Time)		 Call 1 student at a time to the smartboard. The X-Ray shows the 3D Problem → Cure pictures and reads a short 5-word clue ("It has got a tummy ache!"). The student taps the matching pet card (+3, +2, or +1 Star).
4 · Practice B
6 min		 Medicine Cabinet
(2-Word Picture Buttons)		 Call 1 student at a time to the board.
Step 1 (+1 Star): Tap the right Picture + 2-Word Button (💧 Warm water / 🧣 A blanket / 🩹 A bandage / 🍵 Warm tea).
Step 2 (+1 Star): Tap 1 grammar word (has got / Give for Gr 3; should / shouldn't for Gr 4) and the whole class repeats the sentence!
5 · Individual Prep
7 min		 "Circle & Say" Vet Sheet
(At Own Desks)		 No group work! Hand out 1 "Circle & Say" Sheet per student (worksheet.html Page 1 or Page 2). Start the 5-minute timer. Each student works calmly at their own desk: writes 1 pet name, circles 1 pet picture, circles 1 problem picture, circles 1 cure picture, and draws a quick bandage/blanket.
6 · Production
5 min		 Brave Vet Stand-Up & Class Finale Invite 4–5 Brave Vet volunteers to stand at their desk and read the 3 short lines they circled! Tap 🌟 Brave Vet Spoke! (+2 Stars) and 🎲 Spin 1 Easy Question!. Finish with the 🏆 Whole-Class Finale where everyone reads their 3 lines together!
