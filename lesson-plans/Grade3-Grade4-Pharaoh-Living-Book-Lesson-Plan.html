Grade 3 & 4 · Smartboard Master Lesson: The Pharaoh’s Kingdom (Living Pop-Up Book) · 35 minutes

1 × 35 minutes · Classes 3A, 3B, 4A, 4B · Level A1/A1+ · 1 Interactive Smartboard · 4 Teams (Ember, Aqua, Verdant, Astral) · Zero Paper Admin
Core Pedagogical Focus. An immersive 3D Living Pop-Up Book combining ESL speaking fluency, tactile CLIL science (Nile hydrology, pyramid geometry, shadows, papyrus, ancient games), and turn-taking game show mechanics.
Target Language Formulas. “This is a boat. It is on the river.” · “Pyramid B is taller than Pyramid A. Pyramid C is the tallest.” · “These are hieroglyphs. We write our name on papyrus.” · “The palace is next to the pyramid. The guard is behind the pyramid.” · “It’s my turn! / It’s your turn!”
Vocabulary Cluster. pharaoh · pyramid · tomb · Nile · river · boat · taller · bigger · papyrus · scribe · hieroglyphs · cartouche · palace · guard · shield · next to · behind · Senet · turn.
Equipment & Materials. Classroom Interactive Smartboard running pharaoh-living-book.html (100% offline, Web Audio procedural sounds, zero lag), 4 team mini-whiteboards for the Scribe Challenge, student Explorer Passports for wax-seal stamping.

0–5 minPhase 1: Opening the Living BookDisplay the 3D leather-bound book on the oak wood desk. Teacher touches the screen to open Chapter 1. Read the curiosity hook aloud: “Look at the great blue river! What is that wooden boat carrying?” Pupils observe 3D parallax tilt.Pupils echo: “This is a boat. It is on the river.” Teams identify the cargo (sandstone blocks).Calibrate smartboard touch; ensure Web Audio initializes on first tap.
5–11 minPhase 2: Nile Crossing & PyramidsPupils come to the smartboard to row the 3D Cedar Boat across the Nile using the river slider and tap “Stamp My Passport”. Flip to Chapter 2: drag the Sun Slider to see dynamic shadows shift. Measure Pyramids A (10cm), B (15cm), C (20cm) with the golden caliper.Pupils say: “I row the boat! The boat is on the river.” Then: “Pyramid B is taller than Pyramid A! Pyramid C is the tallest!”Emphasize that “taller” is vertical height from ground line; “bigger” is volume.
11–18 minPhase 3: Scribe Workshop & CartoucheFlip to Chapter 3. Introduce the 3D papyrus desk, reed pen, and stone palette. Each team sends a scribe to the board to chisel their team name (e.g. A-Q-U-A, E-M-B-E-R) into the golden cartouche with spark bursts. Tap “Read Scribe Signs”.Team scribes type letters; class chants the phonetic hieroglyphs: “These are hieroglyphs. We write our name on papyrus.”Mini-whiteboards: pupils at their desks write the English letters while team scribe types.
18–26 minPhase 4: Palace Map & 3D SenetFlip to Chapter 4. Move the palace between “Next to” and “Behind” to demonstrate spatial prepositions in 3D depth. Transition to the 3D Senet board: pupils throw the 4 sticks, count points (1–4), move pawns, and model polite turn-taking.Pupils produce target grammar: “The palace is next to the pyramid. The guard is behind the pyramid.” In Senet: “It’s my turn! Throw the sticks! It’s your turn!”Ensure both boys and girls get active turns at the smartboard.
26–33 minPhase 5: 7-Round Star ChallengeFlip to Chapter 5. Run the 7-Round Royal Star Challenge on the smartboard. Each team answers questions on pyramids, tools, language distinctions, and Senet etiquette. Celebrate correct answers with ascending polyphonic chords.Teams confer in 15 seconds, hold up 1, 2, or 3 fingers, and volunteer to tap the board. Group celebration when 7 stars are unlocked.Zero negative feedback: soft descending chime on error with encouraging voice hint.
33–35 minPhase 6: Royal Trophy & CertificateThe 3D Golden Anubis trophy gleams with celebratory rays. Teacher reviews the 5 target formulas with rapid-fire choral chanting. Tap “Print Certificate” to award official Royal Explorer Diplomas to each team.Full chorus: “Welcome to our kingdom! We are ancient Egyptian explorers!” Pupils give team high-fives.+10 XP awarded to all student profiles on the school platform.
