3. Marking and bands

One mark per item. Two answers circled = 0. In section B accept small spelling mistakes if the word is clearly right. Do not take marks off for messy handwriting.

ScoreBandAction
17–20SecureReady for Unit 2. Offer an extension: write 3 sentences, or be a team helper.
11–16DevelopingMove on to Unit 2. Practise the weakest section for 5 minutes in each lesson this week.
0–10Needs supportSmall-group help with picture cards and sentence frames. Repeat the quiz in two weeks.

4. What each section checks

Listening: parachute, claws, frog, kite. Words: Unit 1 vocabulary. Sentences: has got / have got and What does it do? Sounds: sh, fr, magic e. Reading: sequence words.

If most of the class is low in Sounds: repeat the phonics word-card sort. In Sentences: do a has got / have got board drill. In Reading: re-read Mia with First, Second, Third, Last.
